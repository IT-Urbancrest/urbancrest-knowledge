# How do I become a member at Urbancrest?

We'd love to help you explore what it means to be part of the Urbancrest church family. Membership is a commitment to follow Jesus together through worship, spiritual growth, service, and living on mission.

## Where do I start?

**Engage: New Members Class** is a great first step. You'll learn about Urbancrest's history, mission, and vision; explore how church membership works; complete a spiritual gifts assessment; and discover ways to start serving.

You don't have to attend Engage before expressing an interest in membership. You can begin the conversation during a regular worship service or by attending Engage. You're also welcome to complete our **Connect Card** and ask a pastor or staff member about membership.

## What are the requirements for membership?

Church membership is for people who have personally trusted Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior and have been baptized as believers by immersion. If you've trusted Jesus but have not yet been baptized that way, a pastor can help you take that step.

People may seek membership in several situations:

- **A new profession of faith:** If you've decided to follow Jesus, we can talk with you about baptism and joining the church.
- **Coming from another church:** If you're coming from another Southern Baptist church, membership may be requested through a letter from that church.
- **A previous profession of faith and baptism:** If a letter isn't available, a pastor can discuss joining by a statement of faith and prior believer's baptism by immersion at a Baptist church or one of like faith and practice.

There is also a provision for people whose circumstances prevent them from coming forward or attending normally. A pastor can help with those situations individually.

## Is Engage required?

Engage isn't the only way to **start** the membership conversation. Urbancrest's written membership process includes participation in Engage before membership is **completed**. If your circumstances are unusual, or you have questions about the process, talk with a pastor so we can help you understand the appropriate next step.

## What does being a member involve?

Members commit to protecting the unity of the church, praying for and welcoming others, discovering ways to serve, gathering faithfully, and seeking to live in a way that reflects Jesus at home and in the community.

If you'd like to learn more, complete our **Connect Card** and ask about church membership or check our upcoming events for the next Engage class. We'd be glad to walk through the process with you.
