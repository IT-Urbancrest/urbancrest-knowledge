# How do I start following Jesus?

Following Jesus begins with trusting Him as Lord and Savior. The Bible teaches that we've all sinned and cannot make ourselves right with God. Jesus died for our sins and rose again. God offers forgiveness and new life to everyone who turns from sin and places their faith in Jesus. Salvation is a gift of grace, not something we earn.

If you are ready to take that step, you can talk with God honestly, acknowledging your need for Him and trusting Jesus. There aren't special words you have to say to make God accept you. The important thing is repentance and faith in Christ, not repeating a particular prayer.

## What comes next?

Learning to follow Jesus is a lifelong journey. Some good first steps are:

- **Read the Bible.** The Gospel of John is one place to begin learning about Jesus.
- **Pray.** Talk to God honestly about what you're learning, where you're struggling, and what you're thankful for.
- **Gather with other believers.** Worship and grow alongside a local church.
- **Ask about baptism.** Baptism is a public picture of your faith in Jesus, not a way to earn salvation.
- **Keep growing.** Get connected with others who can help you learn to follow Christ.

You don't need to have every question answered before starting a conversation. We'd love to hear your story and help you think through your next step. Use our Connect Card to ask to talk with a pastor. If you've trusted Jesus and want to be baptized, you can complete the Baptism Interest Form.
