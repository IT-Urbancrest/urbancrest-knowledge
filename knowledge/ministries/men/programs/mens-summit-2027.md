# What are the details for Men's Summit 2027?

Men's Summit 2027 is January 22–23 at Urbancrest Church in Lebanon, Ohio. Gather with hundreds of other men of faith for two days of biblical teaching, worship, and encouragement as we grow in our walk with Jesus and sharpen one another.

Hear from Dean Fulks, Ken Bevel, Ted Traylor, Sean McDowell, and Brent Snook. Worship will be led by Charles Billingsley alongside the Urbancrest Worship team.

In-person tickets are $35 and include Friday dinner and Saturday breakfast. Simulcast options are also available. Ticket sales begin October 1, 2026.

## Where is Men's Summit 2027?

The in-person event is at Urbancrest Church, 2634 Drake Road, Lebanon, OH 45036.

## When does Men's Summit 2027 start and end?

On Friday, January 22, doors open at 4:00 PM, dinner begins at 5:00 PM, and worship begins at 6:30 PM. Friday evening concludes at 9:45 PM.

On Saturday, January 23, doors open at 6:30 AM, breakfast begins at 7:00 AM, and worship begins at 8:30 AM. The Summit concludes at 11:00 AM.

## What meals are included with an in-person ticket?

The $35 in-person ticket includes Friday dinner and Saturday breakfast.

Friday dinner is catered by City BBQ and includes pulled pork, green beans, cole slaw, hush puppies, and banana pudding. Drinks include sweet and unsweet tea, lemonade, coffee, and water.

Saturday breakfast includes scrambled eggs, bacon, biscuits, and gravy. Drinks include coffee, orange juice, and water.

## Can I watch Men's Summit 2027 online?

Yes. Men's Summit 2027 offers simulcast tickets for individuals, households, small groups, and churches. Both options include live access and on-demand viewing for 30 days.

| Simulcast option | Viewers | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Individual / Household | 1–10 viewers | $10 |
| Small Group / Church | 11 or more viewers, with no viewer limit | $50 |

## Are Men's Summit 2027 tickets refundable or transferable?

Tickets are non-refundable but may be transferred to another attendee.

## How do I get tickets or find more information?

Ticket sales begin October 1, 2026.
