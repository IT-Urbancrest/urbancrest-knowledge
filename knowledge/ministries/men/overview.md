# What is Men's Ministry?

Urbancrest Men's Ministry, **Battle Ready Brotherhood**, exists to equip men to become the godly men God has called them to be. We want men to be strong in their faith, committed to their families, connected in authentic brotherhood, and prepared for the spiritual battles of everyday life.

Through biblical teaching, **Pursuit Groups**, fellowship, service, and accountability, men are challenged to pursue Christ together. Proverbs 27:17 reminds us, **"As iron sharpens iron, so one man sharpens another."**

The goal is not simply to create more activities for men. Battle Ready Brotherhood seeks to develop **battle-ready disciples** who know their identity in Christ, live with integrity and purpose, lead and serve well, and have brothers beside them who will encourage, challenge, and stand with them.
