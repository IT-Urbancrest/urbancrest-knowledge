# What is the goal of Battle Ready Brotherhood?

Our goal is not simply to create more activities for men. We want to develop **battle-ready disciples**: men who know their identity in Christ, live with integrity and purpose, lead and serve well, and have brothers beside them who will encourage, challenge, and stand with them.
