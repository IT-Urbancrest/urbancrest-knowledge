# What are Pursuit Groups?

**Pursuit Groups** are smaller groups of men who meet regularly to study God's Word, encourage one another, and grow together.

The groups focus on eight areas of biblical manhood:

- **Identity**
- **Integrity**
- **Purpose**
- **Surrender**
- **Passion**
- **Commitment**
- **Compassion**
- **Influence**
