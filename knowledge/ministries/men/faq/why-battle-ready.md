# Why is Urbancrest Men's Ministry called Battle Ready Brotherhood?

Scripture reminds us that our ultimate struggle is spiritual. **Ephesians 6:12** teaches that our struggle is not ultimately against flesh and blood.

Being battle ready means intentionally developing the biblical foundation, spiritual disciplines, relationships, and accountability necessary to faithfully follow Christ in everyday life.
