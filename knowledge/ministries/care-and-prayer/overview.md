# How can I receive prayer or pastoral care?

If you need prayer, encouragement, or someone to talk with, we'd be glad to hear from you.

Our pastors are available to listen, pray, and offer biblical pastoral guidance. If you're going through a particularly difficult season, **Stephen Ministry** offers confidential, Christ-centered, one-to-one support through trained members of our church. Stephen Ministers are trained lay caregivers, not professional counselors or therapists.

## How can I share a prayer request?

You have two options:

- **[Prayer Wall](https://urbancrest.church/prayer):** Share a request for our church family to pray over. Requests are reviewed before appearing **publicly** on the Prayer Wall and may be lightly edited for privacy or clarity. You can choose to display the request anonymously, but it's still a public request once approved.
- **Connect Card:** If you'd like to share a concern **privately** or ask to speak with a pastor, the [Connect Card](https://urbancrest.churchcenter.com/people/forms/1494) is a better next step.

Pastoral care and Stephen Ministry can complement appropriate medical or mental-health care when needed. You can also learn more about [Stephen Ministry](https://urbancrest.church/get-involved/stephen-ministry).
