# What is the Men's Summit?

The Men's Summit grew from a desire to help men grow spiritually, understand biblical manhood, and live it out in today's world.

The first Summit was held in **2005** and welcomed 250 men. It has since grown to reach nearly **1,000 men** and has represented more than **260 churches** from many denominations over the years. Men travel from across Ohio and several surrounding states to attend, and the event has welcomed many nationally recognized speakers.

The Men's Summit is held each year in **late January**. Current dates, speakers, and registration details are available on [Urbancrest Events](https://urbancrest.church/events).
