# Who does the Urbancrest Golf Classic support?

Proceeds from the Urbancrest Golf Classic support one of three ministry partners in rotation:

- **You Turn Ministries:** Shares the gospel, equips leaders, and meets tangible needs in southeast Africa
- **Caring Partners International:** Equips churches to use healthcare to share the gospel
- **A Child's Hope International:** Motivates and mobilizes churches and communities to care for orphans and vulnerable children
