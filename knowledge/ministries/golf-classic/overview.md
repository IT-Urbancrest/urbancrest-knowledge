# What is the Urbancrest Golf Classic?

The Urbancrest Golf Classic began in **1999** and is held each year in **late August**. It is a four-person scramble tournament that raises funds for a ministry partner sharing the love of Jesus Christ.

The supported ministry rotates among three partner organizations:

- **You Turn Ministries**, which shares the gospel, equips leaders, and meets tangible needs in southeast Africa
- **Caring Partners International**, which equips churches to use healthcare to share the gospel
- **A Child's Hope International**, which mobilizes churches and communities to care for orphans and vulnerable children

Current tournament dates and registration details are available on [Urbancrest Events](https://urbancrest.church/events).
