# What is Urbancrest Kids?

Urbancrest Kids exists to equip families to become fully devoted disciples of Jesus, raising children who worship and serve with biblical authenticity and bring transformation to the home, church, and community.

The ministry emphasizes that discipleship is lived out, not only learned. Children grow through relationships with parents, leaders, and peers who model what following Jesus looks like in everyday life. Urbancrest Kids seeks to create a community where children are known, encouraged, taught God's Word, and given opportunities to have fun while growing in faith.

Parents are the strongest spiritual influence in a child's life, so the ministry is committed to serving, supporting, and partnering with families.
