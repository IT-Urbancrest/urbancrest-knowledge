# How are Kids Ministry volunteers screened?

Adult applicants must:

- Attend Urbancrest regularly for six consecutive months before applying to serve with minors
- Complete a written application
- Participate in a face-to-face interview with the Children's Director
- Pass a nationwide criminal background check
- Pass a sex offender registry screening
- Complete required safety and security training
