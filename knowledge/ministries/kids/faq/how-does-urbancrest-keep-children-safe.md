# How does Urbancrest keep children safe in Kids Ministry?

Urbancrest uses several layers of safety, including:

- Screened and trained volunteers
- Multiple leaders in classrooms
- No isolated one-on-one meetings with a child
- Secure check-in and matching-tag checkout
- Ministry leaders and security personnel checking hallways and classrooms
- Two-way radios for immediate assistance
- Attendance records, emergency supplies, and trained emergency procedures

Specific emergency and security tactics are kept with trained staff and volunteers rather than published publicly.
