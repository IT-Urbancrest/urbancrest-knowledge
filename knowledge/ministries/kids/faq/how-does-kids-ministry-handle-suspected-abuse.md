# How does Kids Ministry handle suspected abuse or neglect?

Kids Ministry team members are trained to recognize signs of possible abuse or neglect and to report concerns immediately to appropriate church staff. Volunteers are instructed not to conduct their own investigation, question the child for more information, confront parents, or discuss the concern with other team members.
