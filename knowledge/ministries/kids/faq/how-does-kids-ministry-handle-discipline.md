# How does Kids Ministry handle discipline?

Kids Ministry leaders approach behavior correction with love, patience, and grace. Approved approaches include clear reminders, positive reinforcement, adjusting the environment, and a brief supervised cool-down within the classroom.

The following are prohibited:

- Corporal punishment
- Shouting, mocking, shaming, or humiliating a child
- Isolating a child without supervision
- Withholding bathroom access, water, or necessary snacks

When behavior remains unsafe or disruptive, a ministry leader is contacted and parents are included in a gentle, encouraging conversation.
