# What is AWANA at Urbancrest?

AWANA is a Wednesday-night program where kids learn the Bible and have fun with others in age- and grade-based groups. AWANA stands for **Approved Workmen Are Not Ashamed**.

At Urbancrest, AWANA welcomes **children ages 4 through sixth grade**. During its regular season, clubs meet on **Wednesdays from 6:15 to 7:30 PM**. Kids can join during the year rather than having to wait for a new season.

## Does AWANA cost anything?

There are AWANA dues, but **paying them is not required for your child to attend**. We'd love to have your child participate regardless.

## How do I register?

Registration is available through the current AWANA event in Church Center. Families can also register in person at the **AWANA bookstore in the 300s hallway** on Wednesday evenings. The current event information lists in-person registration at **6:05 PM**.

You're also welcome to join us for **Wednesday Night Dinner at 5:30 PM** in the gym before AWANA.

For the current season's dates, registration link, or any schedule exceptions, check our [Events page](https://urbancrest.church/events).
