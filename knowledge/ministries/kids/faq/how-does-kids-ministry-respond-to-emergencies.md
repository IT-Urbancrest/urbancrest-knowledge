# How does Kids Ministry respond to emergencies?

Kids Ministry volunteers receive procedures for fires, severe weather, lockdowns, evacuations, and family reunification. Classrooms use attendance records and emergency supplies to keep children accounted for, and volunteers follow direction from church leadership, security personnel, and emergency responders.

Children are released only through an approved verification process after leadership or emergency personnel provide clearance. Specific tactical instructions are not published publicly.
