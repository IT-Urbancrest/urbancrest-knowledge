# How does Urbancrest Kids partner with parents?

Urbancrest believes parents are the strongest spiritual influence in a child's life. Kids Ministry supports parents by building relationships with families, communicating with them, and surrounding children with trusted followers of Jesus who reinforce faith at church and at home.
