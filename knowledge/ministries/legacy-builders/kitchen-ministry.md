# Legacy Builders and Kitchen Ministry

Legacy Builders members support the **Kitchen Ministry each week**. Through this ministry, they help provide **more than 1,000 meals each year**, including:

- Wednesday Night Dinners
- Special events
- Funeral dinners

Legacy Builders also uses remaining food from Wednesday Night Dinner to provide meals for people staying at **Bernie's Place**, an emergency shelter in South Lebanon that can house up to 14 individuals.
