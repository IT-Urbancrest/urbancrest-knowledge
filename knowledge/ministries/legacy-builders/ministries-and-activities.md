# Legacy Builders ministries and activities

Legacy Builders is involved in several ongoing ministries and activities:

- **Bears:** Handmade teddy bears are made on the second Tuesday of each month and given to people through nursing homes, mission trips, police departments, and Children's Hospital.
- **Kitchen Ministry:** Legacy members help support the Kitchen Ministry each week, including Wednesday Night Dinners, special events, and funeral dinners.
- **Laundry Ministry:** On the third Wednesday of each month, Legacy helps provide free laundry services and dinner.
- **Bernie's Place:** Through the Legacy Builders Kitchen Ministry, remaining food from Wednesday Night Dinner is taken each week to Bernie's Place, an emergency shelter in South Lebanon that can house up to 14 individuals.
- **Spring Mission Trip:** Legacy takes a four-day mission trip each spring to serve at a children's home, usually in Tennessee, Virginia, or North Carolina.
- **InJoy Adult Sunday School:** The InJoy class meets Sundays at 11:30 AM.
- **Legacy Special Events:** A dinner and program are provided every other month, with music, speakers, and other special programs.
