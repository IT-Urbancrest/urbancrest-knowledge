# Legacy Builders

Legacy Builders is an **active ministry for adults age 50 and older** at Urbancrest. The ministry is led by **Howard and Sue Pauley** and gives adults opportunities to build relationships, serve others, participate in missions, and grow together.

Legacy Builders serves through several ongoing ministries and activities, including handmade teddy bears, Kitchen Ministry, Laundry Ministry, support for Bernie's Place, a spring mission trip, the InJoy Adult Sunday School Class, and special dinner programs throughout the year.
