# Bernie's Place

**Bernie's Place** is an emergency shelter in **South Lebanon** that can house up to **14 individuals** who are experiencing homelessness.

The shelter is named in honor of **Bernie Eichholz**, who spearheaded the effort to make the shelter a reality while in his nineties.

## How Legacy Builders serves Bernie's Place

Legacy Builders serves Bernie's Place through its **Kitchen Ministry**. Each week, remaining food from Urbancrest's **Wednesday Night Dinner** is taken to Bernie's Place to provide meals for the individuals currently staying at the shelter.

This allows food prepared for Wednesday Night Dinner to continue serving people in the community while supporting the ongoing work of Bernie's Place.

## Related ministry

This service is part of the **Legacy Builders Kitchen Ministry**, one of the ways adults age 50 and older at Urbancrest serve the church and community.
