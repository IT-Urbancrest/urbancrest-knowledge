# Legacy Builders Bears ministry

Legacy Builders' **Bears** ministry makes handmade teddy bears on the **second Tuesday of each month**.

Legacy Builders has made **more than 2,000 bears**. The bears have been given through:

- Nursing homes
- Mission trips to children
- Police departments
- Children's Hospital
