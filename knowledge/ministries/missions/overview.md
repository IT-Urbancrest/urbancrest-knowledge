# What is Missions at Urbancrest?

At Urbancrest, we want to help people know Jesus and live on mission, across the street and around the world. Our mission, **Every Nation • Every Street • One Mission**, shapes how we pray, serve, give, and partner with others to share the gospel.

We support missionaries and ministry partners locally, nationally, and internationally. Their work includes church planting, discipleship, leadership training, outreach to students and families, prison ministry, care for children, food distribution, medical needs, and other practical ways to serve people in Jesus' name.

Sharing the gospel and helping people grow as followers of Jesus are central to these partnerships. We come alongside ministry partners through prayer, ongoing financial support, specific projects, and opportunities to serve together.

Everyone has a next step. You can **pray, go, give, and help send others** as we join in what God is doing.
