# What is Missions?

Urbancrest helps people live on mission through both local and global mission engagement.

Global missions creates opportunities for people to participate in mission trips, support mission work, and serve beyond the local community. Local Missions focuses on practical outreach and serving people in and around Lebanon.

Whether the opportunity is across the street or around the world, the goal is to help people faithfully take the gospel to others and serve in Jesus' name.
