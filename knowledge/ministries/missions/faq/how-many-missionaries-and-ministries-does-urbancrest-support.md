# How many missionaries and ministries does Urbancrest support?

Our **2025 missions guide lists 41 missionaries serving in more than 15 countries**. It also highlights **10 Urbancrest outreach ministries and supported local ministries**.

Those local ministries include both ministries of Urbancrest and outside ministry partners.

Together, these relationships connect our church with gospel ministry here in our community, across the United States, and around the world. Support includes ongoing ministry needs and specific projects such as church planting, Bibles, food, clothing, leadership training, and medical needs.

For the latest information about our missions partnerships, contact **Pastor Bob Woods, Interim Global Missions Pastor**.
