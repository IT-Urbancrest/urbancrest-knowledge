# What is the Missions Fund, and how can I give?

The **Urbancrest Missions Fund** helps provide ongoing financial support for missionaries and ministry partners and helps meet specific ministry needs locally, nationally, and internationally.

Missions Fund gifts are given **in addition to regular giving**. **100% of Missions Fund gifts support missions**, including ongoing ministry partnerships and projects such as church planting, Bibles, food, clothing, leadership training, and medical needs.

You can [give online through Urbancrest's giving page](https://urbancrest.churchcenter.com/giving). If you need help directing a gift to the Missions Fund, contact the church office.

A Missions Fund commitment is a prayerful, personal commitment to give toward missions. These commitments help the missions team plan ongoing support for ministry partners. This is a faith commitment between you and the Lord.

For questions about the ministry supported through the fund, contact **Pastor Bob Woods**. For help with giving transactions or records, contact the church office or **Tanya Byrd, Financial Officer**.
