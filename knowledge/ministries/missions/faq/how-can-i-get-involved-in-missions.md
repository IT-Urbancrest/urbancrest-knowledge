# How can I get involved in missions?

There are several ways to take your next step in missions at Urbancrest:

- **Pray:** Pray regularly for missionaries, their families, ministry partners, and the people they serve.
- **Go:** Explore a mission trip or serve with a local outreach ministry.
- **Give:** Support ongoing ministry and specific needs through the Missions Fund.
- **Send:** Encourage those who serve, support their work, and build relationships with our ministry partners through opportunities such as the Missions Conference.

You can also serve close to home through Local Missions. Opportunities include food assistance, furniture and laundry ministries, community outreach, seasonal events, and other practical ways to care for our neighbors.

**Pastor Bob Woods** can help you explore general and global missions opportunities. For Local Missions and community outreach, connect with **Darrel Schick**.

[Explore ways to get involved](https://urbancrest.church/get-involved) or [complete the Volunteer Interest Form](https://urbancrest.churchcenter.com/people/forms/4085).
