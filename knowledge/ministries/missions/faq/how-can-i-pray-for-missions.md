# How can I pray for missions?

Prayer is an important part of how we care for missionaries and participate in their work. Our ministry partners share updates that help us pray for specific people, needs, and opportunities.

You can pray for:

- Missionaries and their families, including their health, encouragement, safety, and spiritual growth.
- People to hear the gospel, trust Jesus, and grow as His disciples.
- Local churches, church plants, ministry leaders, and those being trained to serve.
- Practical needs such as food, housing, transportation, Bibles, and medical care.
- Mission teams, Urbancrest's missions staff, and the people planning and supporting missions work.

A simple place to begin is to pray for one missionary or ministry each day. Our 2025 missions guide includes a monthly prayer schedule to help build that habit. Contact the missions team for current prayer updates and resources.
