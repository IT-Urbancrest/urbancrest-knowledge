# What is the Urbancrest Missions Conference?

The **Urbancrest Missions Conference** is our annual opportunity to celebrate what God is doing through missionaries and ministry partners and discover how we can take part.

During the conference, we welcome ministry partners, hear stories from their work, worship together, and build relationships. It is a chance to learn about the people and ministries we support and consider your own next step through prayer, giving, serving, or going on a mission trip.

Conference activities and schedules vary from year to year. [Visit the Events page](https://urbancrest.church/events) for current information, or contact the missions team with questions.
