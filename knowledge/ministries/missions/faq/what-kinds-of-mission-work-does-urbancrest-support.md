# What kinds of mission work does Urbancrest support?

Urbancrest partners with ministries that share the gospel, give people an opportunity to respond to Jesus, and help them grow as His disciples.

That work takes many forms, including:

- Starting and strengthening churches.
- Training pastors and ministry leaders.
- Reaching students, children, families, and people in the community.
- Serving people in prisons and other outreach settings.
- Caring for children, widows, and families facing practical needs.
- Providing food, clothing, Bibles, medical support, and other ministry resources.

We support both ongoing ministry and specific projects. Practical care and sharing the hope of Jesus work together as we serve people and build relationships.

For questions about missions partnerships, contact **Pastor Bob Woods, Interim Global Missions Pastor**.
