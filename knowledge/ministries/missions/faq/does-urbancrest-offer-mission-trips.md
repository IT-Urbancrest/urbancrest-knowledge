# Does Urbancrest offer mission trips?

Yes. Urbancrest offers opportunities to serve on mission trips in the United States and internationally, working alongside missionaries and ministry partners.

Trips have included community outreach, sharing the gospel, children's and youth ministry, sports and summer camps, home repairs, food assistance, ministry with international students, care for widows and children, and leadership training.

Destinations, dates, costs, and team needs vary by trip. **Pastor Bob Woods** is the primary contact for current mission-trip information and next steps. **Jennifer Prows, Missions Administrator**, can help with administrative questions and coordination.

[Complete the Connect Card](https://urbancrest.churchcenter.com/people/forms/1494) to get connected with the missions team and learn about upcoming mission trips.
