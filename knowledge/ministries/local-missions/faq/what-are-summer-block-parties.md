# What are Urbancrest's Summer Block Parties?

During the summer, the Local Missions Team uses a fully equipped outreach trailer to host block parties at local housing communities, public parks, and the Lebanon KOA campground.

Volunteers are needed to help with setup, teardown, and spending time with guests. These events are designed to build relationships and serve neighbors in a relaxed community setting.
