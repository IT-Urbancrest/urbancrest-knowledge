# Does Urbancrest have pickleball?

Yes. During the colder months, Urbancrest hosts **pickleball in the church gym** as an opportunity for people from the church and community to gather, play, and get to know one another.

The specific dates and times are set on the current calendar, so use the Urbancrest events calendar for the next scheduled pickleball gathering.
