# What other food outreach does Local Missions provide?

Local Missions supports the community through several additional food initiatives.

Urbancrest regularly provides meals for local groups in Lebanon, including teachers, bus drivers, law enforcement, and high school basketball teams. The ministry also partners with and supports the **Lebanon Food Pantry** and packs **Backpacks of Hope** for neighbors experiencing homelessness.
