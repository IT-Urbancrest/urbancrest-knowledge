# What is the Warren County Fair Feeding outreach?

Each **July**, Urbancrest serves meals to hundreds of 4-H families the day before the Warren County Fair opens.

The outreach is a practical way to serve families during one of their busiest days of the year and show Christ's love through hospitality.
