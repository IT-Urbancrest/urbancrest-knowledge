# What are the holiday Baskets of Hope distributions?

Three times each year, at **Thanksgiving, Christmas, and Easter**, Baskets of Hope expands into a larger community event.

These holiday distributions include food assistance along with special meals, movies, prizes, fellowship, and opportunities to build relationships with neighbors.
