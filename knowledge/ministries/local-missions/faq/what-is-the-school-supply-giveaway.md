# What is the School Supply Giveaway?

Each **August**, Urbancrest collects school supplies from church members and distributes them to local school children during a fun, welcoming community event.

Current donation deadlines, requested supplies, and volunteer opportunities may change from year to year, so use the current Urbancrest events calendar for this year's details.
