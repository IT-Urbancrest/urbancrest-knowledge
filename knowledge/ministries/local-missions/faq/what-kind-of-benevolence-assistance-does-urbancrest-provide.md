# What kind of financial or benevolence assistance does Urbancrest provide?

Urbancrest's Local Missions ministry focuses primarily on meeting practical needs rather than providing direct assistance with household bills.

Urbancrest currently:

- Provides food assistance through **Baskets of Hope**
- Offers emergency food boxes in certain situations for people already receiving food assistance who cannot wait until the next distribution
- May provide limited **gas assistance** in some situations
- Does **not** provide assistance with rent, mortgage payments, or utility bills

Our goal is to help where we can while connecting people with the resources Urbancrest is equipped to provide.

For questions about assistance, contact Pastor Darrel at **dschick@urbancrest.org**.
