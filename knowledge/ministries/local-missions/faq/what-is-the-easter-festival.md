# What is the Easter Festival?

The Easter Festival is held **the day before Easter** and is designed to reach and serve the local community.

The event includes games, bounce houses, free Easter baskets, free haircuts, and various giveaways. The goal is to build relationships with neighbors and share Christ with the local community.

For the exact date and event details each year, use the current Urbancrest events calendar.
