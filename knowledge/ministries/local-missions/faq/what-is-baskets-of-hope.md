# What is Baskets of Hope?

Baskets of Hope is Urbancrest's monthly food distribution ministry, providing food assistance to individuals and families in the community.

In addition to the regular monthly distribution, volunteers gather on the other Wednesdays of the month to restock and prepare the pantry.
