# What is the Giving Tree?

The **Giving Tree** is a December outreach designed to give the Urbancrest church family an opportunity to provide Christmas gifts for children in the local community.

Current gift needs and participation instructions may vary each year, so use the current Urbancrest events calendar for the latest details.
