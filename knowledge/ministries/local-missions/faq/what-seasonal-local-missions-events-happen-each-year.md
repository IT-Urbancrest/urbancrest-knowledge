# What seasonal Local Missions events happen each year?

Urbancrest Local Missions serves the community throughout the year through several seasonal outreach efforts:

- **Easter:** Easter Festival and an expanded Baskets of Hope holiday distribution
- **Summer:** Block Parties at local housing communities, parks, and the Lebanon KOA
- **July:** Warren County Fair Feeding for 4-H families
- **August:** School Supply Giveaway
- **October:** Trail of Treats
- **Thanksgiving:** Expanded Baskets of Hope holiday distribution
- **Christmas season:** Giving Tree, expanded Baskets of Hope distribution, and Operation Christmas Child participation

Exact dates and details can change each year, so use the current Urbancrest events calendar for upcoming opportunities.
