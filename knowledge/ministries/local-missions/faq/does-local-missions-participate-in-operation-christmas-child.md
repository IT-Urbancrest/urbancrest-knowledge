# Does Urbancrest participate in Operation Christmas Child?

Yes. Urbancrest participates in **Operation Christmas Child**, partnering with Samaritan's Purse to pack shoebox gifts for children around the world.

Current collection dates and packing opportunities are announced through the Urbancrest events calendar.
