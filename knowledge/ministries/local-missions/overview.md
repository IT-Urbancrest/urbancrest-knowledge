# What is Urbancrest Local Missions?

Urbancrest Local Missions serves neighbors in Lebanon and the surrounding community throughout the year through practical care, relationship-building, and Gospel-centered outreach.

Ministry efforts include food distribution and emergency food assistance, furniture and laundry ministries, community meals, seasonal outreach events, block parties, school supply distribution, the Giving Tree, archery, pickleball, car cruise-ins, Operation Christmas Child, and other opportunities to serve local families and community groups.

The Local Missions Team is always looking for volunteers who want to love their neighbors through practical service and intentional relationships.

For questions or to get involved, contact Pastor Darrel at **dschick@urbancrest.org**.
