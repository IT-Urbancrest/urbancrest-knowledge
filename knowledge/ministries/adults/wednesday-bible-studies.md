# What are Wednesday Night Adult Bible Studies?

Wednesday evenings are an opportunity to study God's Word, build relationships, and grow together with other adults at Urbancrest.

Our regular **Adult Bible Studies begin Wednesdays at 6:30 PM**. You're also welcome to join us for **Wednesday Night Dinner at 5:30 PM** in the gym before the evening activities.

## Which classes are available?

The available studies and their meeting locations can change. To find current options, visit our **[Church Center Groups directory](https://urbancrest.churchcenter.com/groups)** and look under **Weekday Groups**. Each listing is the best place to check the group's current details and any signup instructions.

You don't have to be a Bible expert to explore a group. If you're unsure which option would be a good fit, we'd be glad to help you get connected.
