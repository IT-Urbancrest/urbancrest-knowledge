# Does Urbancrest offer a sensory-friendly worship space?

Yes. A **Sensory-Friendly Worship Space** is available in **The Upper Room** for individuals and families who benefit from a quieter, lower-stimulation environment. A Guest Services team member can help you locate it when you arrive.
