# What is Urbancrest's neurodivergent-friendly worship service?

A neurodivergent-friendly church service offers a setting that helps people with different sensory needs participate in worship. At Urbancrest, our **neurodivergent-friendly (sensory-friendly) worship space** is available in **The Upper Room during the Sunday 11:00 AM service**. It provides a quieter, lower-stimulation environment for individuals and families who benefit from that setting.

If this would help you or someone in your family feel more comfortable joining us, we'd love to welcome you. A **Guest Services team member** can help you find The Upper Room when you arrive.
