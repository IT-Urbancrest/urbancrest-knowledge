# What is Urbancrest's philosophy of worship?

Urbancrest strives to create worship services that are:

- **Christ-centered**
- **Scripture-saturated**
- **Gospel-focused**
- **Centered on congregational participation**

God's Word shapes every service. The congregation is the primary choir, music supports the message, excellence honors God, authenticity matters more than performance, and every generation has something valuable to contribute.
