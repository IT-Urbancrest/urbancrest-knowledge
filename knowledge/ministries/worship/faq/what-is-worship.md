# What is worship?

Worship is our response to God's glory, grace, and greatness. Every believer is called to worship God with all of life, and there is also something uniquely important about God's people gathering to sing, pray, hear His Word, and encourage one another.

At Urbancrest, each part of the service supports the preaching of God's Word and points the congregation to Christ.
