# Do I need to provide snacks for my child in the nursery?

No. The nursery keeps common toddler snacks on hand, such as puffs, yogurt melts, and rice rusks.

If your child has a food allergy, dietary restriction, or you prefer that they not receive a nursery snack, please let the nursery team know at drop-off. Allergy information is also noted for volunteers so it can be reviewed before food is served.
