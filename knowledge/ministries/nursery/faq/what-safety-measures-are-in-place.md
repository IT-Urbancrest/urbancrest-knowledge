# What safety measures are in place in the nursery?

The safety of every child is a priority. Volunteers who serve with children complete an application and interview process, background screening, and ministry safety training before serving.

Urbancrest also follows established multi-volunteer supervision practices so children are not left alone with a single volunteer, and secure check-in and check-out procedures are used when children enter and leave the nursery.
