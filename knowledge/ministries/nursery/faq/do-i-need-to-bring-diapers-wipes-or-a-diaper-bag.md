# Do I need to bring diapers, wipes, or a diaper bag?

The nursery keeps diapers and wipes stocked, so you do not need to provide them unless your child requires a specific brand or product. If so, please let the nursery team know at drop-off and they will use the items you provide.

You are also welcome to leave your diaper bag in the nursery so you do not have to carry it with you throughout the church.
