# How does nursery check-in and check-out work?

Families check in at the designated Kids Ministry check-in stations before bringing their child to the nursery. Children receive a printed name tag, and parents or guardians receive a matching security tag.

At pick-up, the tag numbers must match before a child can be released. If a tag is lost or does not match, a Kids Ministry leader or security team member will assist with the verification process.
