# Stephen Minister training

Stephen Ministers receive in-depth training from their Stephen Leaders using training developed by Stephen Ministries St. Louis.

Training includes active listening, emotional and spiritual support, appropriate boundaries, recognizing when professional referral is needed, and caring for themselves as caregivers. Stephen Ministers also participate in ongoing supervision and peer support throughout their ministry.
