# Stephen Ministry at Urbancrest

Stephen Ministry provides **confidential, Christ-centered, one-to-one care** for people going through difficult times. Trained congregation members, called Stephen Ministers, walk alongside a care receiver by listening, encouraging, praying, and offering a caring Christian presence.

Stephen Ministry is available to people in the congregation and community and serves as an additional care option alongside pastoral care.

Stephen Ministers are trained lay caregivers. They are not professional counselors or therapists.

Learn more about Urbancrest's Stephen Ministry at https://urbancrest.church/get-involved/stephen-ministry.
