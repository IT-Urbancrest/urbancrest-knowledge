# Stephen Ministry and professional counseling

No. Stephen Ministry is **not a substitute for professional counseling, therapy, or medical care**.

Stephen Ministers are trained lay caregivers who provide compassionate, Christ-centered companionship and support. When a care receiver's needs go beyond what a Stephen Minister is equipped to provide, a Stephen Leader or pastor can help identify an appropriate professional next step.
