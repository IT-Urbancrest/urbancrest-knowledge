# Stephen Leaders

Stephen Leaders are trained leaders who provide ongoing leadership and supervision for Stephen Ministry. They help assess the needs of potential care receivers and match them with Stephen Ministers.

Current individual Stephen Leader names are not listed in this knowledge record. A person seeking care should use the Connect Card and select **"I'd like to be contacted by a Stephen Minister."**
