# What a Stephen Minister does

A Stephen Minister is a trained congregation member who walks alongside someone who is hurting. The caring relationship is one-to-one and typically includes regular time together for listening, encouragement, prayer, and Christ-centered emotional and spiritual support.

Stephen Ministers offer a compassionate Christian presence during a difficult season. They are trained lay caregivers, not counselors or therapists.
