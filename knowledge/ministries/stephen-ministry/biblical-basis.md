# Biblical basis for Stephen Ministry

Stephen Ministry reflects the biblical call to **bear one another's burdens** in Galatians 6:2 and to **love one another as Christ has loved us** in John 15:12.

At Urbancrest, the ministry provides a structured way for trained believers to walk alongside people who are hurting with prayer, listening, encouragement, and Christ-centered care.
