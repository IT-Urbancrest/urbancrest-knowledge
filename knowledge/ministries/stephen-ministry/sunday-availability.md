# Sunday Stephen Ministry availability

Each Sunday, **two Stephen Ministry caregivers are available in the back of the Worship Center after each service** for anyone who needs to talk with someone.

For ongoing one-to-one care, a person can also request a Stephen Minister through the Connect Card.
