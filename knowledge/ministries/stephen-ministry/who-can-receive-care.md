# Who Stephen Ministry serves

Stephen Ministry care is available to people in the congregation or community who are going through a life difficulty or crisis.

Situations may include grief and loss, serious illness or hospitalization, divorce or relationship struggles, job loss or financial stress, loneliness or isolation, discouragement, a major life transition, family stress, or caring for an aging parent or family member.

A pastor or Stephen Leader first helps assess the person's needs and determine whether Stephen Ministry is an appropriate care option.
