# Becoming a Stephen Minister

Urbancrest congregation members can serve as Stephen Ministers. If you feel called to care for others and want to be equipped for one-to-one ministry, you can express your interest through Urbancrest's Stephen Ministry page or Connect Card.

Stephen Ministers receive training before serving and continue in ongoing supervision and peer support.
