# What is Guest Services?

The Guest Services team is dedicated to creating a welcoming environment for everyone who comes to worship at Urbancrest. The team helps guests and members feel valued, comfortable, and supported throughout their visit.

Guest Services team members can assist with directions, seating, accessibility needs, and general questions.
