# How can I stay connected with Urbancrest?

There are several ways to find out what's happening and take your next step at Urbancrest.

- **See what's coming up:** Visit [Urbancrest Events](https://urbancrest.church/events) for upcoming activities and gatherings.
- **Get email updates:** Sign up for the [Urbancrest newsletter](https://urbancrest.church/newsletter).
- **Connect with a Small Group:** Browse the [Small Groups directory](https://urbancrest.churchcenter.com/groups).
- **Watch messages:** Visit [Messages](https://urbancrest.church/messages) to watch sermons and find related content.
- **Follow Urbancrest:** Find us on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/urbancrestchurch), [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/urbancrestchurch), and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@urbancrestchurch).

When you want to register for an event, join a group, or submit a form, we'll direct you to the appropriate Church Center option.

New here or have a question? You can complete our Connect Card and let us know how we can help.
