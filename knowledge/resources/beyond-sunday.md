# Beyond Sunday

**Beyond Sunday** is a podcast for and about Urbancrest Church, hosted by **Pastor Geoff Prows and Darrel Schick**.

Join us for honest conversations about faith, life, culture, church, and everything in between. We'll ask real questions, explore what it means to follow Jesus in everyday life, and talk about the things that matter to all of us.
