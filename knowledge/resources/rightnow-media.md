# RightNow Media

## Short Answer

Urbancrest provides free access to RightNow Media, an online library of Bible studies, teaching series, kids content, marriage and family resources, and more. You don't have to be an Urbancrest member to use it.

## Detailed Answer

RightNow Media is a streaming library of Christian video resources for individuals, families, and groups. It includes Bible studies, teaching series, kids content, marriage and family resources, and more.

Urbancrest provides access at no cost to anyone connected to our church. You don't have to be a member. You're also welcome to share RightNow Media access with friends, family, neighbors, or others who could benefit from these resources.

### How do I sign up?

Use Urbancrest's RightNow Media signup link below. On the signup page, click **Sign Up** and enter your information to create an account.

You must be **13 or older** to create a RightNow Media account.

https://app.rightnowmedia.org/en/user/urbancrest

### Where can I watch?

Once you have an account, you can access RightNow Media through:

- RightNowMedia.org
- The RightNow Media mobile app
- Apple TV
- Roku
- Chromecast
- Fire TV

### What if I already have a RightNow Media account?

If your existing RightNow Media account is connected to another church or organization, you'll need to update your organizational affiliation.

In RightNow Media, go to **My Profile → Accounts** and remove your previous organization. Then use Urbancrest's signup/invitation link to connect your account with Urbancrest.

### What if I need help?

For technical support, account issues, or help using RightNow Media, visit the RightNow Media Help Center:

https://help.rightnowmedia.org/

## Urbancrest Information

RightNow Media access is provided by Urbancrest as a free resource for people connected to our church and can be shared personally with others. RightNow Media asks churches not to distribute their signup access to other churches.
