# How do I use Follow Along sermon notes?

**Follow Along** lets you read, fill in, and save your own notes as you listen to an Urbancrest message. You can use it on your phone, tablet, or computer without creating an account.

Go to **https://urbancrest.church/follow-along** to open the current notes. To find earlier messages, choose **Previous Notes**, or visit **https://urbancrest.church/follow-along/archive** to browse and search past notes by title, Scripture, speaker, or series.

## Filling in the notes

- **Tap a blank** to type your answer.
- **Tap the eye icon** beside a blank to reveal its prepared answer.
- **Tap My Notes** to add personal thoughts, questions, or reflections.
- Use **Reveal All Answers** near the bottom to show every prepared answer. You'll be asked to confirm because this will replace unfinished or typed fill-ins with the official answers; your personal notes will remain.
- Choose **Reset My Notes** if you want to clear your saved responses and personal notes for that message on your device.

## Are my notes saved?

Yes, if your browser allows local storage. Your fill-ins, revealed answers, and personal notes save automatically **on the device and browser you're using**. They are not uploaded to an Urbancrest account or synchronized across devices. Clearing your browser's site data or selecting Reset My Notes can remove them. A saving indicator tells you whether they can be saved on that device.

## Can I print or export my notes?

Yes. Choose **Print / Export** to open a preview. From there you can print, or share/download a PDF depending on your device. The PDF includes the **prepared answers** to fill-in blanks and any personal notes you've written, even when you had not revealed those answers on screen. It does not preserve your typed fill-in guesses in place of the prepared answers.

A specific sermon may also include a **Watch this message** link. You can find the message library at **https://urbancrest.church/messages**.
