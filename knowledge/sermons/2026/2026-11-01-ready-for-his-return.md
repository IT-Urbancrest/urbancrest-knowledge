# Ready for His Return

**Speaker** Geoff Prows  
**Date** November 1, 2026  
**Primary Scripture** Matthew 24:42–51 (NASB 1995)  
**Series** Standalone message

## Message Summary

What would change about today if you knew Jesus could return at any time? In Matthew 24:42–51, Jesus tells His followers to stay alert and gives two pictures of what that looks like. A homeowner caught off guard shows why readiness matters. A servant entrusted with a household shows what faithfulness looks like while the master is away.

## Main Idea

Jesus will return, and that promise should shape how we live each day.

## Message Outline

### 1. We Are to Live in a State of Readiness

Jesus says that no one knows the day or hour of His return. Geoff explained that spiritual readiness means keeping eternity in view when daily life fills our attention. It also means pursuing holiness in the choices we make now. Peter makes the same connection in 2 Peter 3:10–11, where the certainty of the Lord’s coming calls believers to holy conduct and godliness.

### 2. We Are to Live Faithfully Until He Returns

Jesus describes a faithful servant who keeps caring for the household entrusted to him. Another servant assumes the master will be gone for a long time, mistreats others, and faces judgment when the master comes back. Geoff used that contrast to ask what Jesus will find us doing when He returns.

Jesus has given every believer work to do. We can share the gospel, help others follow Jesus, use our spiritual gifts to build up the church, and steward our time, resources, and abilities well. As 1 Corinthians 15:58 reminds us, the work we do for the Lord is never in vain.

## A Call to Respond

Readiness begins with trusting Jesus personally as Lord and Savior. If you already follow Him, consider where the promise of His return could change how you live this week. Who could you share the gospel with? Who are you helping grow in faith? What has God already entrusted to you that you can care for faithfully today?
