# Foundations Matter

**Speaker:** Geoff Prows  
**Date:** 2026-09-27  
**Primary Scripture:** Matthew 7:24-29 (NASB 1995)  
**Series:** Summer on the Mount: Kingdom Living for the Here and Now

## Message Summary

In the final message of Summer on the Mount, Geoff Prows turned to Jesus' closing picture in the Sermon on the Mount: two builders, two houses, and two foundations. Both builders hear Jesus' words. The difference is what they do with them. The wise builder hears and acts; the foolish builder hears but does not act.

The rain, floods, and wind strike both houses. Storms do not create the foundation, but they reveal it. Geoff named money, success, relationships, and even religious activity as things people may rely on for security that cannot bear the weight of a life. A life built on Christ and His word has a lasting foundation, even when faith feels weak and circumstances are hard. Jesus holds His people fast.

Jesus' words deserve more than admiration. The crowd was amazed because He taught with His own authority, unlike the scribes who appealed to other teachers. Geoff urged listeners to trust Jesus' authority, receive Him as Lord and Savior, and respond to His teaching with obedience. Obedience is a response to Christ, not a way to earn salvation.

## Main Idea

Foundations matter: hear Jesus' words, trust Him as the solid rock, and put His teaching into practice.

## Message Outline

### I. What Makes a Builder Wise? (Matthew 7:24-27)

The wise builder **hears** Jesus' words attentively and **heeds** them by acting on them. Geoff connected this to James 1:22-25: listening without doing can leave a person deceived about the condition of their life.

The foolish builder also hears, but does not act. The houses may look similar until the storm exposes what lies beneath them. The point is not that following Jesus prevents sickness, sorrow, or disappointment. Rather, Jesus gives His people a firm foundation through those trials. Geoff also pointed beyond present hardships to the final judgment: a foundation that rests on anything other than Christ will not stand.

Geoff invited listeners to consider where they seek security. Jobs, money, relationships, status, and religious routine cannot replace trusting Christ. God's commands are good boundaries, and choosing one's own way instead can bring needless pain and regret.

### II. Why Build on Jesus' Words? (Matthew 7:28-29)

The crowds were **amazed** at Jesus' teaching. He spoke with **authority**, rather than citing a chain of other teachers as the source of His claims. Geoff connected this authority to Jesus' statement in Matthew 28:18 that all authority in heaven and on earth belongs to Him.

The question for each listener is whose authority governs their life. To hear Jesus while setting aside His teaching is to treat personal preference as final. To follow Him is to trust His word enough to obey it, including when obedience is difficult.

## A Call to Respond

As the series closes, Geoff asked listeners to examine the foundation beneath their lives. Those who have not trusted Jesus are invited to turn from sin and self-rule and receive Him as Lord and Savior. Those who belong to Him are called to name an area where they have heard His teaching but have not yet acted, then take a concrete step of obedience with His help.
