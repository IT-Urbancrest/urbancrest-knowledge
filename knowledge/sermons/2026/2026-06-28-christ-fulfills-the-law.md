# Christ Fulfills the Law

**Speaker:** David Bickers  
**Date:** 2026-06-28  
**Primary Scripture:** Matthew 5:17-20 (NKJV)  
**Series:** Summer on the Mount: Kingdom Living for the Here and Now

## Message Summary

Jesus did not come to abolish the Law or the Prophets but to fulfill them. The message emphasized the enduring trustworthiness of God’s Word, the seriousness of obedience for kingdom citizens, and the greater righteousness that only Christ provides.

## Main Idea

The Law reveals our need, and Jesus is its complete fulfillment. The righteousness required for God’s kingdom is not self-produced but provided through Christ.

## Official Fill-In Outline

### 1. Jesus Came to Completely Fulfill

Jesus perfectly fulfilled God’s law and everything the prophets pointed toward.

### 2. Jesus Fulfills Every Part of God’s Word

Every part of God’s Word matters and will be fulfilled.

### 3. Jesus Rewards Those Who Keep His Word

Kingdom citizens take God’s commands seriously and live in faithful obedience.

### 4. Jesus Provides His Own Greater Righteousness

Human goodness cannot meet God’s perfect standard; Christ provides the righteousness sinners need.
