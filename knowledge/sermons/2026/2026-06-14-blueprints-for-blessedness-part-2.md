# God’s Blueprint for Your Blessing

**Speaker:** Geoff Prows  
**Date:** 2026-06-14  
**Primary Scripture:** Matthew 5:7-12 (NASB 1995)  
**Series:** Summer on the Mount: Kingdom Living for the Here and Now

## Message Summary

Jesus completes the Beatitudes by describing kingdom citizens as merciful, pure in heart, peacemakers, and willing to endure persecution for righteousness. These qualities reveal a life changed by God’s grace and shaped by the values of His kingdom.

## Main Idea

The blessed life Jesus describes is visible in how His followers treat others: showing mercy, pursuing purity and peace, and remaining faithful when following Christ brings opposition.

## Official Fill-In Outline

### 1. Blessed Are the Pardoning

Those who have received God’s mercy are called to extend mercy and forgiveness to others.

### 2. Blessed Are the Pure in Heart

God is concerned with the heart, not merely outward religious activity, and calls His people to wholehearted devotion.

### 3. Blessed Are the Peacemakers

Kingdom citizens actively pursue peace and reflect the character of their heavenly Father.

### 4. Blessed Are the Persecuted

Followers of Jesus can remain faithful and rejoice when they suffer for righteousness because their reward is in the kingdom of heaven.
