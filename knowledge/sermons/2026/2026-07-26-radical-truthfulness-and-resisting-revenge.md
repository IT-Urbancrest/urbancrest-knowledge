# Truthfulness and Revenge

**Speaker:** Geoff Prows  
**Date:** 2026-07-26  
**Primary Scripture:** Matthew 5:33-42 (NASB 1995)  
**Series:** Summer on the Mount: Kingdom Living for the Here and Now

## Message Summary

Jesus calls kingdom citizens to radical truthfulness and radical treatment of others. Their words should be dependable without extra oaths, and their response to insult, mistreatment, and personal wrong should reflect grace rather than revenge.

## Main Idea

Christlike righteousness shapes both our speech and our treatment of others: tell the truth with integrity and respond to wrong with grace instead of retaliation.

## Official Fill-In Outline

### 1. Radical Truthfulness to Others

Kingdom citizens should be so consistently truthful that their word does not need extra oaths, promises, or guarantees.

### 2. Radical Treatment of Others

Jesus rejects personal revenge and calls His followers to respond to insult, loss, demands, and need in a way that reflects grace and overcomes evil with good.
