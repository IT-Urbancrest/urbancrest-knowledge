# Faith That Influences

**Speaker:** Geoff Prows  
**Date:** 2026-06-21  
**Primary Scripture:** Matthew 5:13-16 (NASB 1995)  
**Series:** Summer on the Mount: Kingdom Living for the Here and Now

## Message Summary

Jesus calls His followers to be salt and light in a world marked by decay and darkness. Every Christian has influence, and kingdom citizens are called to use that influence so others can see Christ through their lives and glorify the Father.

## Main Idea

Followers of Jesus influence the world by living as preserving salt and visible light, letting the life of Christ be seen through their character and good works.

## Official Fill-In Outline

### 1. Salt to a World in Decay

Like salt preserves, kingdom citizens are called to have a preserving influence in a world marked by sin and decay.

### 2. Light to a World in Darkness

Jesus calls His followers to shine continually so people can see their good works and glorify the Father.
