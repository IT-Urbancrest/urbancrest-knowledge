# When Your Life Becomes a Thank You

**Speaker:** Geoff Prows  
**Date:** November 22, 2026  
**Primary Scripture:** 1 Thessalonians 1:1–10  
**Series:** Standalone message

## Message Summary

When Paul heard how the believers in Thessalonica were living out their faith despite persecution, he thanked God for them. Their lives encouraged him and became an example to others. Geoff asked us to consider whether the way we follow Jesus gives someone else a reason to thank God.

## Main Idea

A life changed by Jesus can become a reason for others to give thanks to God.

## Message Outline

### 1. Faith-filled work

Paul thanked God for their “work of faith” (verse 3). Their actions grew out of their trust in Jesus. We are saved by grace through faith, not by working hard enough to earn God's acceptance. Yet, as Ephesians 2:8–10 teaches, that faith leads us to serve Him and others.

### 2. A labor of love

The Thessalonians' love took effort. Christian love goes beyond kind words: it forgives, serves people who cannot repay us, and keeps caring when doing so is difficult. Jesus showed us this love through His death for us.

### 3. Enduring hope

The church remained faithful under pressure because its hope rested in Jesus. Circumstances could change, but they looked ahead to His return. That same hope helps us keep our eyes on Christ when life is hard.

### 4. A transformed life

The believers followed the example of Jesus, shared the gospel, and turned from idols to serve the living God (verses 6–10). Their past, present, and future had a new direction. God still meets people where they are and changes them from the inside out.

## A Call to Respond

Think of someone whose faith has encouraged you, and thank God for them. Then consider what others see in your life. Does your faith lead you to serve? Does your love take action? Is your hope visible when you face difficulty?

The invitation begins with Jesus. We cannot live like Him until we belong to Him. If you have not trusted Him as Lord and Savior, you can turn to Him today.
