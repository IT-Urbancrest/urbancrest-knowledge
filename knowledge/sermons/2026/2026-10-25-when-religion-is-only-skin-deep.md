# When Religion is Only Skin Deep

**Speaker:** Geoff Prows  
**Date:** October 25, 2026  
**Primary Scripture:** James 1:21-27  
**Series:** Bad Religion, week four (series finale)

## Message Summary

In **When Religion is Only Skin Deep**, Geoff Prows concluded Bad Religion by asking whether our faith reaches beyond outward appearance to a heart changed by Jesus. Religious activity can change what people see without changing who we are. James 1:21-27 shows that authentic faith receives God's Word, puts it into practice, and becomes visible in everyday relationships.

The message identified three things bad religion produces: **hearing without obedience, information without transformation, and activity without fruit**. We can listen to sermons, know Bible stories, and stay busy at church while avoiding what God is calling us to do. Authentic faith grows from a relationship with Jesus and produces the kind of religion James calls pure and undefiled: care for vulnerable people and personal holiness.

Geoff closed by inviting listeners to examine what they are trusting. Church attendance, years of service, and religious knowledge cannot take the place of knowing Jesus. The invitation is to trust Him as Lord and Savior and allow Him to transform us from the inside out.

## Main Idea

**Religion can change what we know and how we appear, but authentic faith in Jesus transforms who we are and how we live.**

## Message Outline

### I. Bad Religion Produces Hearing Without Obedience

In James 1:21-22, receiving God's Word begins with humility and a willingness to put aside sin and selfishness. Geoff encouraged listeners to prepare their hearts throughout the week rather than expecting that preparation to begin when the sermon starts.

Receiving the Word means welcoming what God says and allowing it to take root. As believers grow in spiritual maturity, their desire to obey Jesus should grow too. Knowing or admiring Scripture is not the same as putting it into practice.

James warns that hearing without doing is self-deception. Geoff connected this warning with Jesus' question in Luke 6:46 about calling Him Lord while refusing to do what He says. The question for each of us is where we need to move from listener to doer.

### II. Bad Religion Produces Information Without Transformation

In James 1:23-25, God's Word is like a mirror that reveals our true condition. It exposes our sin, shows us God's holiness, and addresses the thoughts and motives of our hearts. The problem comes when we walk away and forget what God has shown us.

Geoff gave everyday examples of the difference between information and transformation: knowing about forgiveness while remaining bitter, knowing about humility while remaining proud, or knowing about generosity and love while remaining selfish and indifferent to others.

The goal of reading Scripture is to allow God to reshape our lives. Geoff described sanctification as the lifelong process of becoming more like Jesus. Authentic faith brings a changed heart and a continuing direction of growth.

### III. Bad Religion Produces Activity Without Fruit

James 1:25-27 connects a life shaped by God's Word with what it actually produces. Geoff described three areas where outward religion leaves a person lacking:

- **Reality:** Religious activity can create a false sense of security. In Matthew 7:22-23, Jesus warns about people who do impressive things in His name without knowing Him.
- **Restraint:** James 1:26 connects our speech with the condition of our hearts. A changed heart should affect our words, tone, and responses to people. Ephesians 4:29 and Colossians 4:6 call us to speech that builds others up and gives grace.
- **Results:** Empty religion cannot produce lasting spiritual fruit or make us more like Jesus. The question is whether our faith is producing a life that reflects God's character.

Geoff acknowledged that believers stumble. The issue is the overall direction of our lives, including a willingness to repent and grow when God exposes sin.

## What Authentic Faith Produces

James 1:27 describes two expressions of a transformed heart:

### Care for the Vulnerable

James names orphans and widows in their distress. Geoff explained that a heart changed by Jesus becomes a heart of compassion, especially toward people with significant needs. Authentic faith moves beyond knowing that people are hurting to offering practical care.

### Personal Holiness

James also calls believers to keep themselves unstained by the world. Geoff emphasized that holiness is a matter of direction rather than sinless perfection. Followers of Jesus should increasingly desire His character and turn from influences that lead them away from Him.

Compassion and holiness flow from a relationship with Jesus. They do not earn salvation or replace the need to trust Him as Lord and Savior.

## Questions for Personal Reflection

- What has God shown me in His Word that I have not put into practice?
- Am I gathering biblical information while resisting heart transformation?
- What do my words and tone reveal about my heart?
- Am I trusting Jesus or relying on my religious activity?
- Who needs practical care that I could offer this week?
- Is the direction of my life moving toward the character and holiness of Jesus?
