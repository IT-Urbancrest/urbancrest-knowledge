# God is Up to Something

**Speaker:** Geoff Prows  
**Date:** 2026-07-19  
**Primary Scripture:** Habakkuk 2:1-4 (NASB 1995)  
**Series:** Standalone message

## Message Summary

In this standalone message from Habakkuk 2:1-4, Geoff Prows encouraged the church to position itself to hear from God while waiting, trust God with what He says and does, and join Him where He is taking the church.

## Main Idea

When God’s answer does not come immediately, faith waits expectantly, trusts His timing and His word, and responds by joining what He is doing.

## Official Fill-In Outline

**Big Takeaway:** Often times there are spaces between our prayers and God’s answers.

### 1. As You Wait, Put Yourself in a Position to Hear from God

Habakkuk waited and watched for God’s answer rather than simply voicing his complaint and moving on.

### 2. In Faith, Trust God with What He Says and Does

God works according to His appointed time, and faith trusts His word even when the answer is delayed.

### 3. Join in Where God Is Taking Us

The message challenged Urbancrest to respond in faith to where God is leading and to join His ongoing work.
