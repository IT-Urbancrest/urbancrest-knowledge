# A Blueprint for God’s Blessing

**Speaker:** Geoff Prows  
**Date:** 2026-06-07  
**Primary Scripture:** Matthew 5:1-6 (NASB 1995)  
**Series:** Summer on the Mount: Kingdom Living for the Here and Now

## Message Summary

Jesus opens the Sermon on the Mount with the first four Beatitudes, showing that God’s definition of a blessed life stands in contrast to the world’s. Kingdom blessedness begins with spiritual poverty, mourning over sin, gentleness, and a deep hunger and thirst for righteousness.

## Main Idea

True blessedness begins by recognizing our spiritual need, responding to sin with godly sorrow, walking in gentleness, and longing for the righteousness that only God can satisfy.

## Official Fill-In Outline

### 1. Blessed Are the Bankrupt

The poor in spirit recognize their spiritual bankruptcy and their complete need for God’s grace.

### 2. Blessed Are the Bereaved

Those who mourn over sin are promised God’s comfort and are driven toward repentance and grace.

### 3. Blessed Are the Benign

The gentle or meek reflect Christlike strength under control rather than self-assertion.

### 4. Blessed Are the Longing

Those who hunger and thirst for righteousness develop a growing appetite for God and are satisfied in Him.
