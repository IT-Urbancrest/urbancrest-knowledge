# Radical Purity

**Speaker:** Geoff Prows  
**Date:** 2026-07-12  
**Primary Scripture:** Matthew 5:27-30 (NASB 1995)  
**Series:** Summer on the Mount: Kingdom Living for the Here and Now

## Message Summary

Jesus teaches that sexual sin is rooted not only in outward behavior but also in the desires of the heart. Believers are called to guard their hearts, protect their eyes, fill their minds with God’s Word, and take decisive steps away from temptation.

## Main Idea

Radical purity begins in the heart and is pursued by treasuring God’s Word, guarding what we look at, and refusing to make room for temptation.

## Official Fill-In Outline

### 1. Guard Your Heart

Because sin begins within, believers should diligently guard their hearts.

**1. Memorize the Word**  
Treasuring Scripture in the heart helps shape desires and resist sin.

**2. Meditate on the Word**  
Regular meditation on God’s Word directs the mind toward His ways.

### 2. Protect Your Eyes

Jesus calls His followers to take seriously what they continually look at and allow to feed sinful desire.

**1. Stay Away from Temptations**  
Purity requires decisive action to flee temptation rather than make provision for it.
