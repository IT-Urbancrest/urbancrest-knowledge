# Follow Me: The Pathway of Discipleship

**Speaker** David Bickers  
**Date** November 8, 2026  
**Primary Scripture** Matthew 4:18–22 (NKJV)  
**Series** Standalone message

## Message Summary

When Jesus called Simon Peter, Andrew, James, and John, they left their nets and followed Him. Jesus took the lead and promised to make them fishers of men. Pastor Dave used that scene to describe what following Jesus looks like today. As we surrender to Him, His Word shapes our lives, and He teaches us to help others follow Him too.

## Main Idea

Following Jesus is a lifelong journey. At Urbancrest, we practice it together as we Gather, Grow, Give, and Go. Each practice continues as we mature in faith.

## Message Outline

### 1. Gather (Hebrews 10:23–25)

We gather to **worship together** and **build community**. When we worship with other believers, we celebrate what Jesus has done and encourage one another toward love and good works. We need people who will pray with us, challenge us, and help us keep following Jesus through the week.

### 2. Grow (2 Timothy 2:15)

We grow to **deepen our faith** and **know God’s Word**. Scripture shapes us as we read it, study it, and put it into practice. That growth can look like forgiving someone who hurt us, choosing integrity when it costs us, or joining a group where we can study Scripture with others. Pastor Dave also previewed Rooted, a ten-week discipleship study designed to help people grow as followers of Jesus.

### 3. Give (1 Peter 4:10)

We give to **use our gifts** and **serve others**. Jesus washing His disciples’ feet in John 13 shows how love takes the form of service. We can follow His example by giving our time, abilities, energy, and resources to care for people in the church and in our community. Welcoming a guest, preparing a meal, or mentoring a student are all ways to serve.

### 4. Go (Matthew 28:19–20)

We go to **share Jesus** and **make disciples**. The Great Commission sends us to share the gospel in every nation and every street and to help people learn to follow Jesus. That can begin with someone already in our lives. We can share what Jesus has done, open Scripture together, or walk alongside someone as they grow in faith.

## A Call to Respond

Pastor Dave invited listeners to take one of three steps. If you’re beginning to explore faith, respond to Jesus, who died for our sins and rose again. Salvation is God’s gift of grace, received through repentance and faith in Christ. If your faith feels stagnant, bring one area of your life to Jesus and ask Him to help you grow. If you’re ready to invest in someone else, ask God to bring one person to mind whom you can encourage and help follow Jesus.
