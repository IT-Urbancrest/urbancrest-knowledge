# A Great Promise and a Golden Rule

**Speaker:** Geoff Prows  
**Date:** 2026-09-13  
**Primary Scripture:** Matthew 7:7-12 (NASB 1995)  
**Series:** Summer on the Mount: Kingdom Living for the Here and Now

## Message Summary

Pastor Geoff Prows taught that kingdom living requires God's daily grace and enabling power. Because believers cannot develop Christlike character or consistently treat others with grace through their own strength, Jesus invites them to ask, seek, and knock in prayer.

Prayer is not a way to inform or instruct God. It is an invitation into fellowship with Him, a place where He develops His people and teaches them dependence. Jesus promises that the Father hears His children, reveals Himself to those who seek Him, and opens the door to those who keep knocking.

This promise is not permission to name and claim any personal desire. It gives believers confidence to ask according to God's will, especially for the character, wisdom, and grace needed to become more like Jesus. The grace received from the Father then flows outward through the golden rule as believers treat others the way they themselves would want to be treated.

## Main Idea

Believers can pray persistently and confidently because their heavenly Father hears them and gives what is good, then rely on His grace to treat others as they would want to be treated.

## Official Fill-In Outline

### I. A Great Promise to Cling To

God promises to answer prayer. His promise is dependable because it rests on His character and ability, not on human reliability. Jesus gives two ways believers should respond to this promise.

#### 1. Pray Persistently

The commands to ask, seek, and knock describe continuous action. Asking expresses dependence, seeking pursues God Himself, and knocking pictures perseverance until a door is opened. Believers are called to keep praying rather than lose heart.

Persistent prayer includes seeking God's purpose, presence, and power. The greatest problem is often not unanswered prayer but unasked prayer. Jesus does not promise every desire without qualification, but He assures His followers that God hears requests made according to His will and provides what forms Christlike character in them.

#### 2. Pray Confidently

Believers can pray confidently when they remember who receives their prayers. They are not approaching an unknown or distant deity, but their heavenly Father, the sovereign God who welcomes them as His sons and daughters.

Jesus reasons from the lesser to the greater. If imperfect earthly fathers know how to give good gifts to their children, the perfectly good, holy, and righteous heavenly Father can be trusted even more. His love is unselfish, His wisdom makes no mistakes, and His resources never run dry.

### II. A Golden Rule to Follow

Jesus commands His followers to treat people in every situation as they themselves would want to be treated. This includes patience, refusing malicious gossip, listening to understand, honesty and fairness at work, forgiveness during conflict, and grace toward another person's imperfections.

The golden rule flows from the great promise. People cannot consistently live it out unless they first seek God in prayer and receive His grace. It summarizes the heart of the Law and the Prophets by expressing love for God and love for one's neighbor.

## Grace Received and Grace Given

The golden rule is rooted in grace. A person must first receive God's saving grace through faith in Jesus before offering that grace to others. Prows challenged listeners to consider whether they are holding a grudge, withholding forgiveness, or refusing patience that they would hope to receive themselves.

God's grace is greater than every sin and failure. Through Jesus, those who turn from sin and trust Him receive forgiveness, salvation, and a restored relationship with God. Believers then depend on His daily grace to grow more like Christ and extend patience, forgiveness, and love to others.
