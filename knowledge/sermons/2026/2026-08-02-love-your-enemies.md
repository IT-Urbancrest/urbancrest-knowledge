# What To Do With People Who Do Not Love You Back

**Speaker:** Geoff Prows  
**Date:** 2026-08-02  
**Primary Scripture:** Matthew 5:43-48 (NASB 1995)  
**Series:** Summer on the Mount: Kingdom Living for the Here and Now

## Message Summary

Jesus commands His followers to love their enemies and pray for those who persecute them. This countercultural response is a continual choice that reflects the character of the heavenly Father and sets kingdom citizens apart from the world.

## Main Idea

Kingdom citizens do more than love people who love them back. By God’s grace, they continually love enemies and consistently pray for those who mistreat them.

## Official Fill-In Outline

### 1. Love Continually Those Who Do Not Love You

Jesus commands ongoing love toward enemies as a choice rooted in God’s character rather than merely an emotion.

### 2. Pray Consistently for Those Who Do Not Love You

Followers of Jesus are called to bring those who mistreat or persecute them before God in prayer.

**1. We Show We Are Children of God**  
Loving enemies reflects a family resemblance to the Father, who shows goodness to the righteous and unrighteous.

**2. We Set Ourselves Apart from the World**  
Kingdom love goes beyond natural reciprocity and responds differently than the world.
