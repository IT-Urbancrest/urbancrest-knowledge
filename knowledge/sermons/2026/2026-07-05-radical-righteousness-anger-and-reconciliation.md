# Radical Righteousness and Kingdom Relationships

**Speaker:** Geoff Prows  
**Date:** 2026-07-05  
**Primary Scripture:** Matthew 5:21-26 (NASB 1995)  
**Series:** Summer on the Mount: Kingdom Living for the Here and Now

## Message Summary

Jesus moves beyond the outward prohibition of murder to expose sinful anger, contempt, and destructive speech in the heart. He then calls His followers to take broken relationships seriously by pursuing reconciliation with urgency.

## Main Idea

Radical righteousness reaches the heart and our relationships. Jesus calls believers to address sinful attitudes and to pursue reconciliation rather than settling for outward religious obedience.

## Official Fill-In Outline

### 1. We Must Be Aware of Our Sinful Attitudes Not Just Sinful Actions

Jesus exposes heart-level anger and contempt, showing that righteousness involves more than avoiding outward murder.

### 2. God Desires Reconciliation Before Worship

When we know a relationship is broken, Jesus calls us to pursue reconciliation rather than hide behind religious activity.

### 3. There Must Be an Urgency in Reconciliation

Believers should act quickly and do what depends on them to seek peace and right relationships.
