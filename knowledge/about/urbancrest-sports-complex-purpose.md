# Why Urbancrest Is Building the Sports Complex

Urbancrest is developing the Sports Complex as a practical way to **serve the community and build relationships with local families**.

The goal is to create welcoming and accessible spaces where people can gather and participate in recreation while opening new opportunities for the church to care for and connect with the community.

A major part of that vision is serving **families and individuals with special needs**, including through the Miracle League field.
