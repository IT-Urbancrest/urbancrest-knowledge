# Annual Business Meeting

Urbancrest's Annual Business Meeting is held on the **first Sunday of November each year at 6:00 PM**. It's a time for our church family to review plans for the coming year and take part in decisions that support our ministries and mission.

During the meeting, we will:

- Review and vote on the proposed annual budget.
- Vote on candidates for the **Stewardship Team**, **Missions Fund Team**, and **Trustees**.

## Review the proposed budget

You can review the proposed budget at [urbancrest.church/budget](https://urbancrest.church/budget). The page is password protected. You'll find the password in the **printed Connection Guide at the back of the worship center**.

Printed copies of the proposed budget are also available at the back of the worship center.

## Questions about the budget?

Our Stewardship Team would be glad to help. Contact **Tanya Byrd** at [tbyrd@urbancrest.church](mailto:tbyrd@urbancrest.church), and she will help connect you with the team.
