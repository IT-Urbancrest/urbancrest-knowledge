# What should I expect when I visit Urbancrest?

We'd love to have you join us for a time of worship. Our regular Sunday services are at **9:30 AM and 11:00 AM**, and both follow the same format.

Each service includes worship through music, prayer, Scripture, and biblical preaching. You can come as you are. There's no special dress code, and you don't need to know the songs or have all your questions answered to visit.

## When you arrive

Look for the **guest parking spaces near the main front entrance**, to both the left and right of the entrance and closest to the church building. Parking is also available around the building.

The Worship Center is centrally located. If you'd like directions, help finding a seat, or information about a ministry, our Guest Services team would be happy to help.

Seating is open on the main floor and in the balcony. Wheelchair seating is available on the Worship Center main floor, and a quieter sensory-friendly worship space is available in The Upper Room.

## Bringing kids?

Urbancrest Kids offers Sunday programming and nursery care. Kids check-in stations are located in the 300/400 wing. A child receives a name label and the adult receives a matching security tag for pickup. Our team can help you get checked in.

## Coming alone or just exploring?

You're welcome here. You can attend, listen, and ask questions at your own pace. If you'd like someone to meet you or help you find your way, let our Guest Services team know.

Learn more through our [Plan Your Visit](https://urbancrest.church/plan-a-visit) page.
