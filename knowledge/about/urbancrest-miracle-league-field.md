# Miracle League Field

The **Miracle League field** is part of the initial phase of the Urbancrest Sports Complex.

It reflects the project's emphasis on creating an accessible place where athletes with special needs can participate, connect, and experience a sense of belonging.

Specific schedules, registration information, opening dates, and current construction progress should come from current Urbancrest information when those details are available.
