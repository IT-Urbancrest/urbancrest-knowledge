# What time are your services?

## Short Answer

Urbancrest meets every Sunday at **9:30 AM** and **11:00 AM**.

## Sunday Worship

Two identical worship services are offered each Sunday:

- **9:30 AM**
- **11:00 AM**

## Wednesday Evenings

- **5:30 PM** - Free Dinner in the Gymnasium
- **6:15 PM** - AWANA for Kids
- **6:30 PM** - Student Ministry
- **6:30 PM** - Adult Bible Studies

## Schedule Authority

These are Urbancrest's regular weekly service times. If someone asks about a specific holiday or calendar date, check the live event calendar for a documented exception.
