# What are Sunday worship services like?

Urbancrest offers two identical Sunday worship services at **9:30 AM** and **11:00 AM**.

Each service includes worship through music, prayer, and biblical preaching.

For a specific holiday or calendar date, check the live events calendar for any documented schedule exception.
