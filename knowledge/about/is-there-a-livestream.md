# Can I watch Urbancrest services online?

**Yes!** Urbancrest livestreams our **Sunday 9:30 AM worship service** each week.

You can watch live on our [Messages page](https://urbancrest.church/messages), [Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/urbancrestchurch), or [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@urbancrestchurch). You can also find past messages on our website and YouTube channel.

If you can't be with us in person, we're glad you can join from wherever you are. And when you're able, we'd love to welcome you on campus for a time of worship.
