# Sports Complex Phases

## Initial Phase

The initial phase of the Urbancrest Sports Complex includes:

- A **flag football field**
- A **Miracle League field**

## Future Phases

Future phases are planned to include:

- A **wheelchair-accessible mini golf course**
- A **full-size baseball field**

The mini golf course and full-size baseball field are **not part of the initial phase**.
