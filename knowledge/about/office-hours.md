# What are Urbancrest Church's office hours?

## Short Answer

Urbancrest Church's office is open **Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM**.

## Office Hours

- **Monday:** 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM
- **Tuesday:** 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM
- **Wednesday:** 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM
- **Thursday:** 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM
- **Friday:** 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM
- **Saturday:** Closed
- **Sunday:** Closed

The church office is located at **2634 Drake Road, Lebanon, Ohio 45036**.

## If You Mean a Worship Service

Office hours are different from worship service times. Urbancrest's regular Sunday worship services are at **9:30 AM** and **11:00 AM**.

For a vague question such as **"When can I come by the church?"**, explain both the regular office hours and Sunday worship times so the person can tell which option fits what they are looking for.
