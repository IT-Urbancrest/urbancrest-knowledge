# Urbancrest’s Church Affiliation

## Is Urbancrest a Southern Baptist church?

Yes. Urbancrest is a Southern Baptist church. We partner with other Southern Baptist churches to share the gospel, support missions, and serve others.

## What does that relationship look like?

Urbancrest is governed by its own members. No association or convention controls our church or makes decisions for us.

At the same time, we believe in working together with other churches. We cooperate with and support the Cincinnati Area Baptist Association, the State Convention of Baptists in Ohio, and the Southern Baptist Convention. These relationships allow us to encourage one another and support the work of sharing Jesus in our communities and around the world.

Being locally governed and being Southern Baptist go together. Our church makes its own decisions while choosing to partner with churches that share our beliefs and mission.

## What guides Urbancrest’s beliefs?

The Bible is our sole and final authority for what we believe and how we live. Our Statement of Faith explains our understanding of Scripture, and our beliefs are in essential agreement with those of other Southern Baptist churches.

Our affiliation connects us with other churches, while Scripture remains the foundation for our faith and practice.
