# Does Urbancrest believe in transubstantiation?

## Short Answer

**No.** Urbancrest does not believe that the bread and cup become the literal body and blood of Jesus during the Lord's Supper. We understand the elements as **symbols that help believers remember Christ's sacrifice**.

## What Is Transubstantiation?

Transubstantiation is the belief that the substance of the bread and wine changes into the actual body and blood of Christ while their outward appearance remains the same.

Urbancrest does not hold this view.

## What Does Urbancrest Believe Happens During Communion?

The Lord's Supper is a **symbolic act of obedience and remembrance**. The bread represents Christ's body given for us, and the cup represents His blood shed for us.

Believers participate in Communion to remember Christ's sacrifice, proclaim His death, examine themselves, and look forward to His return.

## Are the Bread and Cup Literally Jesus' Body and Blood?

**No.** Urbancrest understands Jesus' words at the Last Supper within the symbolic and memorial nature of the ordinance. The bread and cup point us to the body and blood of Christ, but they do not change in substance into His physical body and blood.

## Detailed Answer

Urbancrest does not affirm the doctrine of transubstantiation.

We believe the Lord's Supper was given by Jesus as a symbolic act of obedience for His followers. Through the bread and the cup, believers remember His body given for them and His blood shed for the forgiveness of sins.

Communion directs our attention to the once-for-all sacrifice of Jesus, calls believers to examine themselves, proclaims His death, and reminds us that He is coming again.
