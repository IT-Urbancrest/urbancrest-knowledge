# What does Urbancrest believe about suicide?

## Short Answer

Every human life is precious because every person is made in God's image. Suicide is a tragedy that should be met with compassion, truth, and care.

## Detailed Answer

Urbancrest does not teach that suicide is the unforgivable sin. Salvation rests in the finished work of Jesus Christ and is received by grace through faith. At the same time, Scripture calls us to value and protect human life, so suicide should never be treated as God's desire or as a solution to suffering.

## Urbancrest Information

If this question is connected to someone who is currently considering suicide or self-harm, immediate safety and crisis support should come before an ordinary doctrinal conversation. Urbancrest pastors are also available to provide pastoral care and support.
