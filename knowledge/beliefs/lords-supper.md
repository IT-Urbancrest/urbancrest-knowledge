# What does Urbancrest believe about the Lord's Supper?

## Short Answer

The Lord's Supper, also called Communion, is an ordinance given by Jesus in which believers remember His sacrifice and proclaim His death until He returns.

## Who May Participate

Communion at Urbancrest is for **baptized followers of Jesus**. You do not need to be a member of Urbancrest to participate.

## Baptism

**Yes.** Urbancrest practices close communion, so we ask that those participating in the Lord's Supper have trusted in Jesus and followed Him in believer's baptism. Church membership is not required.

## If You Have Not Been Baptized

We ask that you **wait to participate until after believer's baptism**. Urbancrest practices close communion, which means the Lord's Supper is for baptized followers of Jesus.

## Church Membership

**No.** You do not need to be a member of Urbancrest to participate in Communion. If you are a follower of Jesus and have been baptized, you are welcome to participate in the Lord's Supper.

## Visitors and Guests

**Yes.** Visitors and guests are welcome to participate if they are followers of Jesus who have been baptized. You do not need to be an Urbancrest member.

## What Close Communion Means

**Close communion** means Urbancrest invites baptized followers of Jesus to participate in the Lord's Supper. Participation is not limited to Urbancrest members.

## Open, Close, and Closed Communion

Urbancrest practices **close communion**. We ask participants to be followers of Jesus who have been baptized, but we do not require them to be members of Urbancrest.

## Detailed Answer

Urbancrest practices **close communion**. The Lord's Supper is for those who have trusted in Jesus Christ for salvation and have followed Him in believer's baptism.

Church membership at Urbancrest is not required.

If you have not yet trusted Christ or have not yet been baptized, we ask that you refrain from taking the elements. Participating in the Lord's Supper is an opportunity for believers to remember Christ's sacrifice, examine themselves, and proclaim the gospel together.
