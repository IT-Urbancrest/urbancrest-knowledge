# What does Urbancrest believe about the Lord's Supper?

## Short Answer

The Lord's Supper, also called Communion, is an ordinance given by Jesus in which believers remember His sacrifice and proclaim His death until He returns.

## Detailed Answer

Urbancrest practices **close communion**. The Lord's Supper is for those who have trusted in Jesus Christ for salvation and have followed Him in believer's baptism.

**You do not need to be a member of Urbancrest to participate.**

If you have not yet trusted Christ or have not yet been baptized, we ask that you refrain from taking the elements.

We understand the Lord's Supper as an ordinance for baptized followers of Jesus. Participating is an opportunity for believers to remember Christ's sacrifice, examine themselves, and proclaim the gospel together.
