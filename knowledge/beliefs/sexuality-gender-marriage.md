# What does Urbancrest believe about sexuality, gender, and marriage?

## Short Answer

Urbancrest believes **every person is created in the image of God** and therefore possesses God-given dignity and is worthy of respect, compassion, and Christian love.

We also believe God created humanity male and female, that marriage is the covenant union of **one man and one woman for life**, and that God intends sexual intimacy for marriage.

Because of those convictions, Urbancrest does not affirm same-sex sexual relationships or a view of gender that separates a person's identity as male or female from God's created design. Disagreement with someone's beliefs or choices never removes that person's dignity or our responsibility to treat them with love and respect.

## What Does Urbancrest Believe About Marriage?

Urbancrest believes marriage is the covenant union of **one man and one woman for life**. Marriage is God's design for the intimate relationship between husband and wife and provides the biblical context for sexual intimacy.

## What Does Urbancrest Believe About Sexuality?

Sexuality is part of God's good creation, but Scripture also gives boundaries for how sexual desires and relationships should be expressed.

Urbancrest believes sexual intimacy is intended for marriage between one man and one woman. This standard applies to everyone and is part of the broader Christian call to submit every area of life to Jesus.

## What Does Urbancrest Believe About Gender?

Urbancrest believes God created human beings **male and female**, both equally made in His image and possessing equal dignity and worth.

We therefore understand being male or female as part of God's created design rather than an identity that is ultimately self-defined apart from the body God has given us.

## What Does Urbancrest Believe About Same-Sex Relationships?

Urbancrest does not affirm same-sex sexual relationships or same-sex marriage because we believe Scripture reserves sexual intimacy for marriage between one man and one woman.

At the same time, this conviction does not make same-sex attraction or sexual sin an uniquely unforgivable category. Like every person, each of us needs the grace, forgiveness, and transforming work of Jesus Christ.

## How Does Urbancrest Treat People Who Identify as LGBTQ?

Every person should be treated with **dignity, compassion, respect, and Christian love**.

People are not reduced to a label, attraction, struggle, or disagreement. We want to speak truth without hostility and show the same patience and grace that every one of us needs from Christ and from one another.

## Can Someone Who Identifies as LGBTQ Attend Urbancrest?

**Yes.** People from every background are welcome to attend Urbancrest, hear the gospel, ask questions, and learn what it means to follow Jesus.

We do not require someone to already agree with everything we believe before walking through the door.

## Detailed Answer

Urbancrest's beliefs about sexuality, gender, and marriage begin with two biblical convictions held together.

First, **every human being is created in the image of God** and therefore has immeasurable worth. Every person should be treated with dignity, compassion, respect, and Christian love.

Second, we believe God has given a good design for human sexuality. God created humanity male and female, established marriage as the covenant union of one man and one woman, and intends sexual intimacy for marriage.

For that reason, Urbancrest does not affirm same-sex sexual relationships, same-sex marriage, or a view of gender that separates male or female identity from God's created design.

These convictions do not change the church's responsibility to welcome people, speak with humility and compassion, and point every person, including ourselves, to the grace and truth of Jesus Christ.
