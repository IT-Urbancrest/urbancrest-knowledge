# What does Urbancrest believe about the Rapture?

## Short Answer

Urbancrest believes Scripture teaches that **believers will be gathered to Christ**. Those who have died in Christ will be raised, and believers who are alive will be **caught up together with them to meet the Lord**.

Christians commonly call this event the **Rapture**.

## What Is the Rapture?

The Rapture refers to the gathering of believers to Jesus described most clearly in **1 Thessalonians 4:13-18**.

Paul teaches that when the Lord comes, the dead in Christ will rise and believers who are still alive will be caught up together with them to meet the Lord. **1 Corinthians 15:51-52** also teaches that believers will be changed at the resurrection.

The Rapture is therefore not mainly about escaping the world. It is about the hope of being united with Christ and sharing in the resurrection He has promised.

## Is the Word "Rapture" in the Bible?

The English word **rapture** does not appear in most Bible translations, but the teaching it describes does.

In 1 Thessalonians 4:17, Paul says believers will be **"caught up"** to meet the Lord. The term *Rapture* developed as a way of referring to that biblical event.

## When Will the Rapture Happen?

Scripture clearly teaches that believers will be gathered to Christ, but Christians have held different views about how that event relates to the Tribulation and other end-times events.

Urbancrest's doctrinal framework does not make a detailed pre-, mid-, or post-tribulation timeline a required belief. We should be careful not to claim certainty where Scripture has led faithful Christians to different conclusions.

What is certain is that **Jesus will return, the dead will be raised, believers will be gathered to Him, and God's purposes will be fulfilled**.

## Is the Rapture the Same as the Second Coming?

Christians use these terms in different ways.

Some understand the Rapture and Christ's visible return to earth as distinct events within the fulfillment of His promised coming. Others understand the gathering of believers as part of the same climactic return of Christ.

Urbancrest's core doctrinal emphasis is not on requiring one detailed timeline. It is on the truths Scripture makes clear: **Christ will return personally and visibly, believers will be raised and gathered to Him, all people will face judgment, and Christ will reign in victory**.

## How Should Christians Respond to Teaching About the Rapture?

The Bible presents Christ's return as a source of **hope, encouragement, readiness, and faithful living**, not fear or endless speculation.

Paul closes his teaching about believers being caught up to meet the Lord by telling Christians to **encourage one another with these words**.

Our confidence is not in predicting every detail of the end times. Our confidence is in Jesus Christ and His promise to return for His people.
