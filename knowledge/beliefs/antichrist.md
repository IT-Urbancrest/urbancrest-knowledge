# What does Urbancrest believe about the Antichrist?

## Short Answer

Scripture warns that opposition to Christ is already at work in the world and also points to a **future personal and climactic opponent of Christ**, commonly referred to as the Antichrist. Christians should take these warnings seriously without becoming consumed by speculation.

## Who Is the Antichrist?

The New Testament uses the term **antichrist** for those who deny or oppose Christ and says that a spirit of antichrist is already at work in the world.

Scripture also describes a future figure of extraordinary rebellion and opposition to God, including the "man of lawlessness" in 2 Thessalonians 2. Urbancrest understands these passages to point to a future personal and climactic opponent of Christ.

## Does the Bible Teach That There Will Be a Final Antichrist?

**Yes.** Urbancrest understands Scripture to teach that opposition to Christ will culminate in a final personal opponent before Christ's ultimate victory.

The Baptist Faith & Message does not require a detailed end-times timeline or identify the Antichrist by name. Its central emphasis is that Jesus Christ will return personally and visibly, humanity will be judged, and God's purposes will be fulfilled.

## Can We Know Who the Antichrist Is Today?

Scripture does not give Christians permission to confidently identify a contemporary political, religious, or public figure as the Antichrist.

Christians should be discerning about false teaching and opposition to Christ, but speculation about a particular person's identity can distract from the Bible's clearer commands to remain faithful to Jesus.

## How Should Christians Respond to Teaching About the Antichrist?

Christians should be **watchful, discerning, and faithful**, but not fearful or obsessed with speculation.

Our confidence is not in correctly predicting every detail of end-times events. Our confidence is in Jesus Christ, His promised return, and His final victory over sin and evil.

## Detailed Answer

Urbancrest believes Scripture teaches that opposition to Christ is already present in the world and will ultimately culminate in a final personal opponent of Christ.

We do not believe Christians are called to continually identify contemporary candidates for the Antichrist or build their faith around speculative end-times predictions. The emphasis of biblical teaching about the last things is the certain return, judgment, reign, and victory of Jesus Christ.

Because Christ wins, believers can face the future with faithfulness and hope rather than fear.
