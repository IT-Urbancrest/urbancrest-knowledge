# Why does God allow suffering?

Pain and suffering raise some of the hardest questions we can ask about God. The Bible doesn't pretend suffering is easy, and it doesn't give us a simple explanation for every tragedy.

Scripture teaches that we live in a world affected by sin and brokenness. It also shows us that God has not remained distant from our pain. Jesus suffered, died on the cross, and rose again. Because of Him, Christians can have hope even while they are grieving, questioning, or waiting for answers.

This doesn't mean every painful event is a punishment for someone's wrongdoing. Jesus challenged that assumption in John 9:1–3. Nor does believing in God mean we never feel sadness or uncertainty.

The Bible promises that God will ultimately judge evil and make all things new. Until then, we can bring our questions and pain to Him and care for one another through difficult seasons.

If you're walking through something painful, we'd be glad to listen and pray with you. Urbancrest's pastors and Stephen Ministry are available as care options.
