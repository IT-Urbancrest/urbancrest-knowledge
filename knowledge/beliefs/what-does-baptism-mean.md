# What does baptism mean?

## Short Answer

Baptism is a public declaration that a believer belongs to Jesus.

## Who Should Be Baptized

Baptism is for someone who has personally trusted in Jesus Christ. It follows salvation as an act of obedience and a public declaration of faith.

## Does Baptism Save

**No.** Baptism does not cause salvation. It follows salvation and publicly identifies a believer with Jesus.

## Why Immersion

Urbancrest practices baptism by immersion. Immersion pictures the believer's union with Christ in His death, burial, and resurrection.

## Detailed Answer

Baptism is a public declaration that a believer belongs to Jesus. Immersion pictures the believer's union with Christ in His death, burial, and resurrection. Baptism follows salvation and does not cause salvation.
