# What is sin and why does it matter?

The Bible uses the word **sin** to describe our rebellion against God and our failure to live according to His good and holy ways. Sin isn't limited to the worst things people do. It reaches into our choices, attitudes, desires, and our failure to love God and others as we should.

Scripture teaches that everyone has sinned. Sin separates us from God, and we cannot repair that separation by trying to be good enough. That's why the good news about Jesus matters: He lived without sin, died for sinners, and rose again. Forgiveness and new life are God's gifts to those who repent and trust Christ.

Christians don't talk about sin to claim they're better than anyone else. We all need God's grace. If you'd like to understand what it means to turn to Jesus, we'd love to help you explore the gospel.
