# What does Urbancrest believe about heaven, hell, and life after death?

Christians have real questions about death, eternity, heaven, and hell. At Urbancrest, we look to Scripture for what we can say with confidence.

The Bible teaches that **Jesus Christ will return**, that the dead will be raised, and that God will judge all people. Those who belong to Christ will live forever with God. Those who reject Him face eternal judgment. God promises to make all things new.

## What is heaven?

The Christian hope is more than escaping this life. It is everlasting life with God through Jesus Christ, culminating in God's promised renewal of creation. Revelation 21 describes a future in which God dwells with His people and death and mourning are no more.

## What does Urbancrest believe about hell?

**Hell is a real place of everlasting punishment, eternal darkness, and separation from God.** Scripture describes hell as *outer darkness* and speaks of eternal punishment and separation from the presence of the Lord (Matthew 8:12; Matthew 25:46; 2 Thessalonians 1:9). Hell is not simply a metaphor for suffering in this life, and its judgment is not temporary.

We take this teaching seriously because Jesus Himself spoke about judgment and eternity. The Bible also clearly teaches that God offers forgiveness and eternal life through repentance and faith in Jesus Christ.

## Can I know where I stand with God?

The gospel's promise is that forgiveness and eternal life are received through repentance and faith in Jesus Christ, not through our own good works. If you have questions about salvation or eternity, we'd be glad to talk with you.
