# How can I know God is real?

It's okay to ask whether God is real. Christians believe that God has made Himself known through what He has created, through Scripture, and most clearly through Jesus Christ.

Creation can prompt questions about why anything exists at all. The Bible also speaks of a moral awareness that points beyond ourselves. But at the center of Christian faith is Jesus: His life, teaching, death, and resurrection. Christians believe Jesus shows us who God is.

Faith doesn't require pretending we never have questions. If you're exploring Christianity, one place to start is reading one of the Gospels, such as John, and asking: Who does Jesus claim to be? What did He do? Why did His followers believe He rose from the dead?

You can explore these questions at your own pace. We'd be glad to connect you with a pastor if you want to talk about faith, evidence, or doubts.
