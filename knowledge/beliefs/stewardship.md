# What does Urbancrest believe about stewardship?

## Short Answer

Everything belongs to God, and Christians should faithfully use their time, abilities, possessions, and finances for His glory.

## What Stewardship Includes

Stewardship is about more than money. Christians are called to faithfully use their **time, abilities, possessions, and finances** for God's glory.

## How Christians Should Give

Giving should be **willing, generous, and joyful**, not driven by pressure or obligation.

## Why Giving Matters

Faithful giving supports the church's ministry, care for others, and the spread of the gospel.

## Detailed Answer

Everything belongs to God. Christians should faithfully use what He has entrusted to them for His glory. Giving should be willing, generous, and joyful, supporting ministry, care for others, and the spread of the gospel.
