# What does Urbancrest believe about the Trinity?

## Short Answer

There is **one God who eternally exists as three distinct Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit**. Each Person is fully God, yet God is one.

## Is the Trinity Three Gods?

**No.** Christianity teaches that there is only one God. The Father, Son, and Holy Spirit are not three separate gods. They share the same divine nature and are eternally one God.

## Are the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit the Same Person?

**No.** The Father, Son, and Holy Spirit are distinct Persons. The Father is not the Son, the Son is not the Holy Spirit, and the Holy Spirit is not the Father. Yet each is fully and eternally God.

## What Does "Three in One" Mean?

The Trinity means that God is **one in being and three in Person**. God has eternally revealed Himself as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit without division of His nature or essence.

## Detailed Answer

Urbancrest believes in one living and true God who eternally exists as **Father, Son, and Holy Spirit**.

The three Persons of the Trinity are distinct from one another, but they are not divided in their divine nature. The Father is God, the Son is God, and the Holy Spirit is God, yet there is only one God.

The Trinity is not the belief that there are three gods, nor that one Person simply appears in three different forms. Father, Son, and Holy Spirit eternally exist together as the one triune God.
