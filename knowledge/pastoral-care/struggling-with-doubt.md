# What if I’m struggling with doubts about my faith?

If you're wrestling with questions about God, the Bible, or Christianity, you don't have to pretend you aren't. Questions can be an opportunity to examine what you believe and look more closely at Jesus.

Start by naming the question that's troubling you. Is it something you've experienced, a part of Scripture that's hard to understand, or a question about whether Christianity is true? Read what the Bible says in context, pray honestly, and talk with a trusted Christian who will listen rather than rush you toward an easy answer.

We won't claim that every question has a quick explanation. But you don't have to work through every doubt alone. If you'd like to talk, use Urbancrest's Connect Card to request a conversation with a pastor.
