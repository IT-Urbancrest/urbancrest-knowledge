# What if I’ve been hurt by a church before?

If you've been hurt, dismissed, or mistreated in a church setting, returning to church may feel complicated. We're sorry that happened, and we don't want to minimize your experience or ask you to pretend it didn't matter.

You are welcome to ask questions about Urbancrest, attend a service without committing to anything, or talk with a pastor before visiting if that would be helpful. You can take time to learn what our church believes and how we care for people.

We can't undo what happened elsewhere, and we won't promise that any church is perfect. But we'd be glad to listen if you want to begin a conversation. You can reach out through our Connect Card.
