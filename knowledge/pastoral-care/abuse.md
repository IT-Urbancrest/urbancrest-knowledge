# Abuse and unsafe relationships

If someone is hurting, threatening, controlling, or abusing you, your safety matters. If you are in immediate danger, move to a safer place if you can and contact local emergency services.

You do not need to confront the person causing harm by yourself. Urbancrest pastors can listen and provide pastoral support, but church care should not replace emergency, legal, medical, or professional help when those services are needed.
