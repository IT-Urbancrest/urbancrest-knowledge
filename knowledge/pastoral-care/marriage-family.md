# Marriage and family crisis

Urbancrest pastors are available to listen, pray with you, and provide biblical pastoral care when a marriage or family is under strain.

**Stephen Ministry** is also available alongside pastoral care for confidential, Christ-centered, one-to-one support through trained lay caregivers. A Stephen Minister can listen, encourage, pray, and walk alongside someone through a difficult season. Stephen Ministers are not professional counselors or therapists.

A difficult relationship situation may also benefit from qualified professional counseling or other appropriate support. If abuse, threats, or immediate danger are involved, safety comes before ordinary marriage counseling.
