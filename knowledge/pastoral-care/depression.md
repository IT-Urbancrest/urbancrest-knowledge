# Depression and emotional distress

If you are struggling with depression or emotional distress, Urbancrest pastors are available to listen, pray with you, and offer pastoral care.

Pastoral support can be part of a broader support system that may also include trusted family or friends and qualified medical or mental-health professionals.

If you are thinking about suicide, self-harm, or not wanting to live, immediate crisis support should take priority.
