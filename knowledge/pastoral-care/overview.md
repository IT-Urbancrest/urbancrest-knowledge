# Pastoral care at Urbancrest

## Short Answer

Yes. Urbancrest pastors are available to listen, pray with you, and provide biblical pastoral care. **Stephen Ministry** is also available alongside pastoral care for confidential, Christ-centered, one-to-one support through trained lay caregivers.

## More Information

Pastoral care is available for people who need prayer, spiritual guidance, or support through a difficult season. When ongoing one-to-one care would be helpful, Stephen Ministry can provide a trained lay caregiver who listens, encourages, prays, and walks alongside the care receiver.

Stephen Ministers are not professional counselors or therapists.

Learn more about Stephen Ministry at https://urbancrest.church/get-involved/stephen-ministry.
