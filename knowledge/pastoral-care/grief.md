# Grief and loss

Grief can be heavy and deeply personal. Urbancrest pastors are available to listen, pray with you, and walk alongside you through loss.

You do not have to have everything figured out before asking for support. Reaching out to trusted family, friends, and appropriate professional support can also be helpful as you grieve.

If grief includes thoughts of harming yourself or not wanting to live, immediate crisis support should come before ordinary pastoral-care follow-up.
