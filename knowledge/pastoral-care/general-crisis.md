# Personal crisis

If you are going through a difficult personal situation, Urbancrest pastors are available to listen, pray with you, and provide pastoral care.

If the situation involves immediate danger, suicidal thoughts, self-harm, violence, abuse, or a medical emergency, immediate safety support should come before ordinary pastoral follow-up.
