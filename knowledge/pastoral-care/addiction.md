# Addiction

If you are struggling with addiction, Urbancrest pastors are available to listen, pray with you, and help you take a next step without shame.

Pastoral care can work alongside trusted accountability and appropriate professional or medical support. If stopping a substance could create a medical emergency, seek medical help rather than trying to manage that risk through church advice alone.
