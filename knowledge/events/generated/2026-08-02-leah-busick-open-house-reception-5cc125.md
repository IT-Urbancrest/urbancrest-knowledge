**When:** Sunday, August 2, 2026, 4 PM to 6 PM

## Details

Join us as we celebrate 
Leah Busick
 and her faithful ministry at Urbancrest Church!
After serving as our 
Kids Ministry Director for the past 15 years
, Leah is beginning an exciting new chapter as 
Vice Principal at Lebanon Christian Schools
. We are incredibly grateful for the countless ways she has invested in children, families, and our church, and we'd love for you to help us thank her and wish her well.
Stop by for a time of fellowship, encouragement, and celebration on 
Sunday, August 2, from 4:00–6:00 PM
 in the 
Urbancrest Gymnasium
. 
Coffee, cake, and donuts
 will be provided.
Whether you served alongside Leah, your family was impacted by her ministry, or you'd simply like to express your appreciation, we hope you'll join us as we celebrate God's faithfulness through her years of service and pray for her as she begins this new season.
