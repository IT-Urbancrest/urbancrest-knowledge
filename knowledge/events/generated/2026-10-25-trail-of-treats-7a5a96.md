****************
Trail of Treats:
****************

Bring the whole family to Urbancrest’s Trail of Treats for an evening of candy, treats, and outdoor fun. Costumes are welcome, but not required.

Sunday, October 25, 5:00–7:30 PM
Urbancrest Church
2634 Drake Rd.
Lebanon, OH 45036

This is a free, outdoor event, so please dress for the weather.

Attending? No registration is required. Just come and enjoy the evening!

Volunteering or bringing a Trunk or Treat vehicle? Volunteers and vehicle hosts do need to register for their serving area. Volunteer opportunities include:

* Trunk or Treat
* Food and Block Party Stations
* Greeters
* Parking Lot Helpers

Trunk or Treat vehicle hosts are encouraged to decorate their trunks and bring their own candy. Donated candy will be distributed to vehicles that need more throughout the evening.

Questions? Contact the Urbancrest office at office@urbancrest.org.
