# Baking Spirits Bright: Christmas Cookie Exchange

Ladies, pull out your favorite Christmas cookie recipe and join us for our first Christmas Cookie Exchange!...

**When:** Sunday, December 6, 2026, 5:30 PM to 7:30 PM

**Where:** Urbancrest Church - 2634 Drake Road, Lebanon, OH 45036

Ladies, pull out your favorite Christmas cookie recipe and join us for our first Christmas Cookie Exchange! We’ll sample each other’s cookies, enjoy light refreshments, and take home a variety of Christmas cookies.
