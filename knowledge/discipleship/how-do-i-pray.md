# How do I pray?

Prayer is talking with God. You don't need a special vocabulary, a certain posture, or perfectly worded sentences. You can come to Him honestly with what is on your heart.

If you aren't sure where to begin, try talking with God about these things:

- **Praise:** Tell God what you know is true about Him.
- **Thanks:** Thank Him for His goodness and for the things He has provided.
- **Confession:** Be honest about sin and ask Him for forgiveness.
- **Requests:** Ask for help, wisdom, and the needs of others.

Jesus taught His disciples how to pray in Matthew 6:9–13. The Lord's Prayer is a helpful model because it begins with God's name and purposes, includes our daily needs, asks for forgiveness, and expresses dependence on Him.

Prayer doesn't have to be long. You can pray on your way to work, with your family, before reading Scripture, or during a difficult moment. God isn't impressed by polished words. He wants us to come to Him with sincere hearts.

If you'd like someone at Urbancrest to pray with you, you can share a request on our [Prayer Wall](https://urbancrest.church/prayer). Requests there are reviewed before appearing publicly, so use the **Connect Card** instead if you'd prefer to share something privately or ask to speak with a pastor.
