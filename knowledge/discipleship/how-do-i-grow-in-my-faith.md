# How do I grow in my faith?

Growing in faith isn't about having everything figured out. It's about learning to know Jesus, trust Him, and follow Him in everyday life.

At Urbancrest, we talk about making disciples who **Gather, Grow, Give, and Go**. These are practical ways to live out our faith:

- **Gather** for worship, biblical teaching, prayer, and time with other believers.
- **Grow** by reading Scripture, praying, asking questions, and building relationships that encourage you to follow Jesus.
- **Give** by practicing generosity and using what God has entrusted to you to serve others.
- **Go** by loving your neighbors, sharing the hope of Jesus, and participating in His mission wherever you are.

You don't have to do everything at once. Start with one next step: join us on Sunday, explore a Small Group, begin reading the Bible, or ask about an opportunity to serve.

If you're unsure where you fit, complete the Connect Card. We'd love to help you find a place to grow.
