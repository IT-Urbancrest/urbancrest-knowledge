# How do I start reading the Bible?

If you've never read the Bible before, you don't have to start on page one and read straight through. A great place to begin is the **Gospel of John**, which introduces Jesus through His words, actions, death, and resurrection.

A simple way to get started is to:

1. **Read a short passage.** A chapter or a few paragraphs is enough.
2. **Ask what it says.** Who is speaking? What is happening? What does it teach about God and people?
3. **Think about how to respond.** Is there something to believe, confess, thank God for, or obey?
4. **Pray about what you've read.** Ask God for understanding and help putting His Word into practice.

You don't need one particular English Bible translation to participate at Urbancrest. Pastor Geoff commonly preaches from the NASB and Pastor Dave typically uses the NKJV, but other faithful translations can be helpful as you read and learn.

If you get confused, that's okay. Write down your questions and ask someone at church. Reading with a friend or a Small Group can also help you understand Scripture in context.
