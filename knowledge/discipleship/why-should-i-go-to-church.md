# Why should I go to church?

Following Jesus is personal, but it isn't meant to be lived alone. The New Testament describes believers gathering to worship, learn from Scripture, pray, care for one another, and serve together.

At Urbancrest, we gather on Sundays for worship through music, Scripture, prayer, and biblical preaching. There are also opportunities to build relationships through Small Groups, ministry gatherings, serving, and missions.

You don't have to be a church member, have a perfect life, or know all the right words before coming. If you're exploring Christianity, you're welcome to come, listen, ask questions, and get to know people.

Our regular Sunday services are at **9:30 AM and 11:00 AM**. You can learn more on the Plan Your Visit page.
