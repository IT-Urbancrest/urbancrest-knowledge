# Pavilion

Urbancrest's pavilion is available to reserve on a limited basis. Church members may request a reservation. Outside agencies may also request use of the pavilion, subject to Urbancrest's discretion and availability.

The pavilion has men's and women's restrooms and a fireplace. Contact the church office to ask about availability and reservation details.

Alcohol is not permitted anywhere on Urbancrest's property, including at the pavilion.
