# Outdoor Activities at Urbancrest

We have a beautiful 70-acre campus, and we're glad to share our outdoor spaces with our community. You're welcome to fish in the pond, play disc golf, or ask about reserving the pavilion.

- **Pond:** The pond is open to the public for catch-and-release fishing. We ask that you do not fish during church services on Sundays.
- **Disc golf:** Our nine-hole course, also called Frisbee golf, is open to the public on a first-come, first-served basis.
- **Pavilion:** Church members can request a reservation, and outside agencies may request use at Urbancrest's discretion. Availability is limited. The pavilion has men's and women's restrooms and a fireplace. Contact the church office to ask about reservations.

Alcohol is not permitted anywhere on Urbancrest's property.
